The questions people ask before paying.

掏钱之前，大家都会问的

What happens to my data if I downgrade?降级之后我的数据会怎样？

Nothing is deleted. Plan limits govern what is published, never what is stored: on a smaller plan your website shows fewer works, and every record stays exactly where it was. Move back up and the same content is public again.

什么都不会删。套餐上限管的是**发布**，不管存储：换到更小的档位，你的官网少展示几件作品，所有记录原封不动。换回去，同样的内容重新公开。

Is there a contract or a minimum term?有合同或最短期限吗？

The plan is monthly. The setup fee is one-time and is not refundable once the migration has been delivered and signed off, because by then the work is done.

月费按月计。Setup 服务费是一次性的；数据迁移交付并经你签字确认之后不退，因为那时工作已经完成。

Can I see the product before paying anything?付钱之前能先看到产品吗？

Yes. Book a walkthrough and we will show you a working studio — the public website, the console, the timetable and the family side — with real data in it, not slides.

可以。预约一次演示，我们会带你看一个真实运转的工作室——公开官网、后台、课表和家长端，里面是真数据，不是幻灯片。

Do you take a percentage of my fees?你们会抽成吗？

No. There is no commission, no per-enrolment fee and no payment processing cut — the product does not process payments at all.

不会。没有佣金、没有按报名收费、也没有支付通道抽成——产品根本不经手支付。

Is the website included, or does it cost extra?官网是包含的，还是要另外付费？

Included, on every plan, with hosting. It is not an add-on: the public website reads the same records your staff work in, which is the reason the timetable on it can never disagree with the timetable they schedule.

包含，三档都有，含托管。它不是附加项：公开官网读的就是员工日常操作的那份数据——这也正是官网上的课表不可能和他们排的课对不上的原因。

What if I have more students than the plan allows?学员数超过套餐上限怎么办？

Move up a plan. Nothing breaks at the boundary and nothing is deleted; the console tells you which limit you are near before you reach it.

换到更高一档。到达上限时不会有任何东西损坏，也不会删数据；后台会在你接近上限之前告诉你。

Can I export my data and leave?我能导出数据然后离开吗？

Yes. Students, enrolments, attendance and credit history export as files you keep. We would rather you could leave easily than stay because you cannot.

可以。学员、报名、签到与课时账本都能导出成你自己保存的文件。我们宁愿你随时能走，也不希望你因为走不了而留下。

Is the product available in Chinese?产品有中文吗？

Both languages, everywhere — the console, the family portal and your public website, which serves each language on its own address. Bilingual is a property of the product, not a translation layer bolted on.

全线双语——后台、家长端，以及你的公开官网（每种语言有自己的网址）。双语是产品的属性，不是事后加的翻译层。

Still deciding? The full user manual is public, and so is the machine-readable pricing file.

还在权衡？完整用户手册是公开的，机器可读的价格文件也是。
