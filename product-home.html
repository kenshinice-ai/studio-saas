Three decisions三个关键设计

Money and trust belong in the system, not in goodwill.

钱和信任，写进系统，不写在人情里

None of the three below is a bullet on a feature list. They are what decides whether a studio can hand this system its money and its families.

下面三件事没有一件是功能清单上的卖点，它们是决定这套系统能不能被托付的地方。

The ledger cannot be edited

账本不可篡改

A balance is the sum of its entries, not a number somebody can type over. Every movement records who, when and why. Check-in deducts the credit automatically, and an insufficient balance is stopped at the door rather than discovered at month end.

余额 = 每一笔流水之和。不是一个可以随手改掉的数字，每次变动都记录操作人、时间与原因。签到自动扣课时，余额不足当场拦截并提醒。

Permissions live in the backend

权限写死在后端

Owner, manager, teacher, front desk, staff. The front desk can take a payment and cannot issue a refund; refunds and outbound sharing are the owner’s and manager’s alone. It is not a hidden button — the server refuses. That moves the pressure of saying no off your staff and onto the system.

店主 / 店长 / 老师 / 前台 / 员工，五级权限。前台能收费不能退费，退费与对外分享仅限店主店长，不是在界面上藏起来，是系统层面直接拒绝——把人情压力从员工身上移到系统上。

Minors’ privacy

未成年人隐私

Over 200 tenant-isolation checks must pass before any release ships. Publishing a child’s work requires a recorded guardian consent, and consent and withdrawal are an append-only chain of events. Share links expire in 1–90 days and can be revoked at any time.

两百多项租户隔离测试每次发版全绿才允许上线；作品公开须记录监护人同意，同意与撤回是只增不改的事件链；分享链接 1–90 天强制到期，可随时撤销。

Is there a free trial? 有免费试用吗？

No. Instead of a fourteen-day empty account, the evaluation is a 30-minute walkthrough in which we build a previewable portal live, using your studio’s own name, courses and work. You are looking at your studio before you decide, not at a demo studio.

没有。我们不给一个空着的十四天账号，而是用一次 30 分钟的演示：现场用你工作室的名字、课程和作品，生成一个可预览的门户。你在决定之前看到的是自己的工作室，不是样板间。

What does the setup fee not cover? Setup 费用不包含什么？

It covers brand configuration, one reviewed migration of your student list and class-credit balances, team training and launch support. Outside it: implementation beyond that scope, migration clean-up, messaging provider fees, payment processing, custom domains and multi-campus aggregation. Each is scoped and quoted separately, and the final terms follow the signed order form.

包含的是：品牌配置、学员名单与课时余额的一次性经审核迁移、团队培训、上线支持。不包含：超出该范围的实施、数据清洗、消息供应商费用、在线支付、自定义域名与多校区汇总。以上各项单独约定并单独报价，最终以签署的订单为准。

Nobody on our team is technical. Is that a problem? 我们团队没有懂技术的人，行不行？

No. The four launch steps above are done with you, not handed to you, and they are covered by the setup fee. After that each role only ever learns its own screen — the front desk takes a payment, a teacher checks students in, the manager reads the books. Nobody has to understand the whole system to use their part of it.

可以。上面的四步是我们陪着做，不是丢给你做，且已包含在 Setup 费用内。上线之后，每个角色只需要学自己那一屏——前台收费、老师签到、店长看账。没有人需要先弄懂整个系统才能用上自己的那部分。

If we leave, do we keep our data? 如果我们不用了，数据还是我们的吗？

You keep your rights in your data and in the brand material you supply; we hold a limited right to host and process it in order to run the service. On termination a standard export is made available — the window for it, and the notice period for ending a monthly subscription, are set in the order form rather than asserted on this page. The full clause is in the terms of service.

是。你的数据与你提供的品牌素材，权利始终归你；我们只有为提供服务而进行托管与处理的有限权利。终止后我们提供标准导出——导出窗口期，以及月度订阅的终止通知期，写在订单里，而不是由这个页面来承诺。完整条款见服务条款。

Where do our students’ records live? 学员资料存放在哪里？

In Sydney, on Australian infrastructure (ap-southeast-2). Publishing a child’s work requires a recorded guardian consent, share links expire and can be revoked, and over 200 tenant-isolation checks must pass before any release ships. The service FAQ answers this in full, including the gaps — there is no multi-factor authentication yet, and backups do not have an off-box copy.

在悉尼，澳大利亚境内（ap-southeast-2）。公开一名儿童的作品需要留痕的监护人授权；分享链接会过期、也可随时撤销；每次发布前必须通过 200 多项租户隔离检查。服务 FAQ 里有完整回答，也包括还没做到的部分——目前还没有多因素认证，备份也还没有异地副本。

We have more than one campus. 我们有多个校区。

Each campus runs as its own tenant, and that is a decision rather than a limitation: student records, staff membership, schedules, brand, plan — and backup, export, deletion and incident scope — stay independent per campus. Reporting across campuses is scoped separately, and does not merge their operational data.

每个校区作为独立租户运行。这是刻意的设计，不是能力上限：学员记录、员工成员、排课、品牌、套餐——以及备份、导出、删除与事故范围——按校区彼此独立。跨校区的汇总报表单独约定，且不会合并各校区的运营数据。
