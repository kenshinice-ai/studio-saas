Since v8.1.0

v8.1.0 之后

Everything below shipped to production between 1 and 6 August 2026. Each line is deployed, not planned.

以下内容均已于 2026 年 8 月 1 日至 6 日发布至生产环境。每一条都已上线，而非计划。

One palette and one colour you choose, instead of eight palettes chosen for you (v8.5.0, extended in v8.5.1)一套配色 + 一个你自己定的颜色，取代八套替你选好的配色（v8.5.0，v8.5.1 扩展）

The product shipped eight colour themes, one recommended per industry. Removing them fixed a defect nobody had measured: because the PAPER carried the industry's hue, whichever status colour shared that hue stopped being visible. A green theme could not show "saved"; a blue one could not show a notice. Five of the seven light themes had one of these. The paper and the ink are now constant, and so are success, warning, danger and info — they mean the same thing in every studio, so they look the same in every studio.

产品此前有八套配色主题，每个行业推荐一套。取消它们的同时修掉了一个从没有人量过的缺陷：因为**纸**带着行业的色相，哪个状态色跟纸同色相，那个状态色就看不见了。绿色主题显示不出「已保存」，蓝色主题显示不出提示。七套浅色主题里有五套中招。现在纸与墨是固定的，成功、警示、危险、提示四个状态色也是——它们在每一间工作室含义相同，因此长得也相同。

What a studio sets instead is one colour, normally taken straight out of its own logo. Only the hue of that colour is used: the depth and the intensity are solved by the system against measured contrast targets. A fluorescent logo becomes a proper deep green rather than a call to action nobody can read, and a colour that would be mistaken for a status is moved out of the way and says so. An industry template still changes the vocabulary, the section headings and the enrolment questions — it just no longer repaints the site.

工作室要定的只剩一个颜色，通常直接从自己的 Logo 里取。系统**只取它的色相**，深浅与浓度由系统对着实测的对比度目标解算。荧光色的 Logo 会变成一支体面的深绿，而不是一个没人看得清的按钮；会被误读成状态色的颜色会被挪开，并明确告诉你挪了。行业模版依然会更换词汇、板块标题与报名问题——只是不再重新粉刷整个网站。

The public site also got its shape back. Every corner on it was 2 or 4 pixels, which reads as a form; it now uses a five-step soft scale and two levels of elevation, and the hero image carries a single hand-drawn organic edge. This changes no colour at all — it was simply the largest visual difference between this product and the studio sites it is meant to look like.

对外官网也找回了它的形状。此前页面上每一个圆角不是 2 像素就是 4 像素，那读起来像一张表单；现在是五档柔和圆角与两级投影，首屏图片带一处手绘般的有机边缘。这没有改动任何颜色——它只是这个产品与它想要对标的画室网站之间，最大的一处视觉差距。

v8.5.1 answered the first thing owners said about it: the colour choice had stopped looking like a choice. There is now a row of seven starting colours to pick from before the free picker, the industry cards no longer carry a swatch bar promising a palette they no longer set, and the hero image can be cut to a rectangle, an oval or the organic shape rather than always the last one.

v8.5.1 回应了工作室的第一个反馈：颜色的选择不再像一个选择了。取色器之前现在有一排七个起始色可选；行业卡片不再带那条承诺配色的色带（它已经不再决定配色）；首屏图片也可以选择方形、椭圆或艺术异形，而不是永远只有最后一种。

One colour system instead of nine, and dark mode reaching the parts a palette cannot (v8.4.0, extended in v8.4.1 and v8.4.2)九套配色合并成一套，深色模式覆盖到配色管不到的地方（v8.4.0，v8.4.1 与 v8.4.2 扩展）

The public studio site has had eight solved colour themes for a while. The rest of the product did not: the studio console declared 45 colours of its own, 33 of them stock framework defaults sitting on warm paper they were never designed for; the platform console had a second, different set; the password page and the shared portfolio page each had a third and a fourth. Seven palettes in one product, sharing eight token names that meant different things in each. That is why dark mode could never simply be switched on — there was no single thing to switch. All of them now come from one generator, which checks 976 colour pairs on every build.

对外官网早已有八套解算出来的配色主题，产品的其余部分没有：工作室控制台自己声明了 45 个颜色，其中 33 个是框架默认色，压在为它们设计的暖色纸张上；平台控制台是另一套；设密码页与作品集分享页各是第三、第四套。一个产品里七套配色，共用八个在各处含义不同的 token 名。深色模式一直开不起来，原因就在这里——没有一个「它」可以开。现在全部由同一个生成器产出，每次构建校验 976 组颜色配对。

Dark mode itself was failing in places no palette could reach. Scrollbars, dropdown lists, checkboxes and the browser's autofill are drawn by the browser and read no colour setting; on a dark studio site they all stayed light. The back-to-top button was a fixed cream circle under text that goes light in dark mode, so the arrow was invisible on all eight dark themes. The phone address bar stayed cream above a near-black page. All fixed, and each is now checked.

深色模式本身还在一些配色管不到的地方出错。滚动条、下拉列表、复选框与浏览器自动填充由浏览器绘制，不读任何颜色设置，深色官网上它们全是浅色。「回到顶部」按钮是固定的米色圆形，而其上的文字在深色下会变浅，所以八套深色主题里那个箭头都是看不见的。手机地址栏在近黑色页面上方仍是米色。均已修复，并已纳入检查。

A studio can now set its site to follow each visitor's device, so a parent opening the enrolment page at night sees the dark palette. Both palettes are published when that is switched on. And uploading a hero photo now actually shows it — the upload filled in the address but left a separate dropdown on "Soft Art Board", so the photo was saved, published, and never displayed.

工作室现在可以将官网设置为跟随访客设备，夜里打开报名页的家长会看到深色配色；开启后两套配色都会发布。另外，上传首屏照片现在真的会显示——此前上传只填好了地址，而另一个下拉框仍停在「柔和画板」，于是照片保存了、发布了，却从不显示。

v8.4.1 carried the same work into the operations CMS, which turned out to have a hand-built dark palette with the cards drawn darker than the surface beneath them — the defect the studio themes had fixed a release earlier, still live here because this was a separate set nobody regenerated. The student registration page had a ninth palette of its own. Both now load on the same paper as everything else. Dark cards also sit further off the page than they did: the amount they lifted was correct as arithmetic and too small to see, so it is now measured the way a person perceives it rather than the way a number reads.

v8.4.1 把同样的工作带到了运营 CMS —— 那里有一套手写的深色配色，卡片竟然比它下面的背景更暗；这正是官网主题在上个版本已修复的问题，因为这是另一套没人重新生成过的配色而一直留着。学员报名页还有属于它自己的第九套配色。两者现在都与其余部分使用同一套底色。深色下的卡片也比之前更明显地浮起来：原先的抬升数值在算术上正确，但看起来太小，现在按人眼的感知来度量，而不是按数字读数。

v8.4.2 found why the CMS had resisted every earlier attempt. It is built with utility classes generated in the browser, and the fix so far had been a layer of override rules chasing those classes after the fact — a layer that reached just over half of them and had to grow every time a component was added. It now configures the generator instead, so all of them follow the studio's palette. The rule that had been impossible to express: the grey scale has to flip between light and dark, because a pale grey is a background in one and text in the other, while a red button stays a red button in both.

v8.4.2 找到了运营 CMS 一直修不好的原因。它由浏览器中即时生成的工具类构成，而此前的做法是用一层覆盖规则去追这些类——这层规则只盖到了其中一半多一点，而且每加一个组件就得再长一截。现在改为直接配置生成器，于是全部工具类都跟随工作室的配色。此前无法表达的那条规则是：灰阶必须在明暗之间翻转，因为浅灰在一种模式下是背景、在另一种下是文字；而红色按钮在两种模式下都是红色按钮。

A workable studio console, and dark themes that stack the right way (v8.3.0, corrected in v8.3.1)可用的工作室控制台，以及排列正确的深色主题（v8.3.0，v8.3.1 修正）

The Website & Brand console spent more than half of a laptop screen on itself before the first control: a title bar, a section title repeating it, and a panel of description repeating that. On a phone the first editable field sat more than a full screen down, and nothing stayed pinned while you scrolled — not the tab you were editing under, not the Publish button. Two of those layers are gone and the third is a single row; the phone now pins the tab strip and the publish bar. Ninety-four controls that were too small to tap reliably have been brought up to size.

「官网与品牌」控制台在显示第一个可操作控件之前，占掉了笔记本屏幕的一半以上：一条标题栏、一个重复它的版块标题、再加一块重复该标题的说明面板。在手机上，第一个可编辑字段位于一整屏之外，且滚动时没有任何东西固定——正在编辑的标签页没有，发布按钮也没有。其中两层已删除，第三层压缩为一行；手机端现在会固定标签栏与发布条。九十四个过小、难以准确点击的控件已全部调整到标准尺寸。

Separately: all eight dark colour themes were arranged upside down. The alternating band behind a section came out brighter than the cards sitting on it, so the cards read as holes. Every colour in them passed its contrast requirement, which is why the checks had always been green — contrast says whether text can be read, not which surface should look nearer. All eight are re-solved, and the check that runs on every build now tests the arrangement as well as the contrast.

另外：八套深色主题的层次全部是反的。版块背后的间隔带比压在它上面的卡片还亮，卡片因此看起来像凹陷的洞。这些配色的每一项对比度都是达标的——这正是此前检查一直通过的原因：对比度只说明文字能否被读清，不说明哪个表面应该显得更靠前。八套配色已全部重新求解，每次构建运行的检查现在除对比度外也会校验层次排列。

Industry presets: the wording shown on an industry card and the wording published to the live site were two separate texts, and in Chinese five of the eight had drifted apart — the card promised one headline and the site went live with another. They are one text now. The registration page headings and intros were also rewritten so that both languages say the same kind of thing, and the intro names the questions that will actually be asked.

行业预设：行业卡片上显示的文案与实际发布到官网的文案，此前是两段各自独立的文字，其中中文有五个行业已经出现分歧——卡片承诺的是一句标题，官网上线的却是另一句。现已合并为同一段文字。报名页的标题与引导语也一并重写，使中英文表达同一类内容，并在引导语中直接写明将会询问哪些问题。

Correction to the previous release (v8.2.31)上一版本的修正（v8.2.31）

The v8.2.30 release briefly showed a block of program code across the top of the platform console, and the date check it was meant to add was not actually active. Both are corrected. No customer-facing page was affected and no data was involved.

v8.2.30 版本曾在平台控制台顶部短暂显示出一段程序代码，且该版本本应新增的日期校验实际并未生效。两者均已修正。未影响任何面向客户的页面，也不涉及任何数据。

Saving a studio works again, and the subscription dates now mean something (v8.2.30)工作室保存恢复正常，订阅日期开始有实际意义（v8.2.30）

Editing a studio that already had a Studio Admin login had been failing since 10 July: the save returned an error and changed nothing at all, unless a new password was typed each time. That is fixed. The four subscription dates are also checked against each other now — a trial that ends before the subscription starts, or a cancellation dated before the period it cancels, is refused with a sentence saying which two dates disagree, instead of being stored. And a new view lists every subscription that has passed one of its dates: a trial that ran out, a billing period that lapsed, a cancellation date that went by. Nothing in the product read those dates before; a studio could pass all three and the console still showed green. The list reports only — moving a studio is a separate, deliberate action, and a lapsed trial is always left for a person to decide.

编辑一间已配置管理员登录的工作室，自 7 月 10 日起一直失败：保存会报错，并且什么都没有改到，除非每次都重新输入一遍密码。此问题已修复。四个订阅日期现在会互相校验——试用结束早于订阅开始、取消日期早于它所取消的周期，都会被拒绝并明确指出是哪两个日期冲突，而不再被存进去。另外新增一个视图，列出所有已经越过某个日期的订阅：试用已结束、计费周期已过、取消日期已到。此前产品里没有任何代码读取过这些日期，一间工作室三项全都越过，控制台依然显示正常。该列表只做呈现——调整工作室状态是另一个需要明确执行的动作，而试用到期永远留给人来决定。

Platform console: two data-loss defects, and the family identity (v8.2.29)平台控制台：两处数据丢失，以及品牌统一（v8.2.29）

Opening a studio in the platform console, changing anything, and pressing Save cleared all four subscription dates. The dates arrived from the API in one format and were read as another, so every date field rendered empty and the empty value was what got saved. A second, separate path cleared the trial end date on every save regardless. Both are fixed, and the server now keeps a date it was not asked to change. The console also received the studio identity it never had — warm paper, navy ink, one amber accent instead of five unrelated colours — and the tenant detail view became five tabs instead of one wall of cards that rendered seven of its fields twice.

在平台控制台打开一间工作室、改动任何内容、点击保存，四个订阅日期会全部被清空。日期从接口传来是一种格式，页面按另一种读取，于是每个日期框都显示为空，保存时写回去的就是这个空值。另有一条独立路径，无论如何都会在每次保存时清掉试用结束日。两处均已修复，服务端现在会保留没有被要求修改的日期。控制台也终于用上了本该有的品牌视觉——暖色纸面、藏青文字、单一琥珀强调色，取代此前五种互不相关的颜色——工作室详情由一整面卡片墙（其中七个字段重复渲染了两次）改为五个分页。

Findable, and readable by machines (v8.2.28)可被检索，也可被机器读取（v8.2.28）

Every WebP image on the site — including the one used as the link preview — was being served with a file type that says “unknown binary”. Browsers guessed correctly, which is why nothing looked broken; social platforms do not guess, so a shared link showed no picture. Fixed, along with caching: the manual’s screenshots were re-downloaded on every visit and are now held by the browser. The site also gained a sitemap, a robots file, and machine-readable pricing at /pricing.md and /llms.txt — the pages an AI assistant reads when a studio owner asks it what to use. The service FAQ, terms, privacy and support pages now each have a Chinese address of their own instead of a language switch inside one page.

站点上所有 WebP 图片——包括用作链接预览的那张——此前都以「未知二进制」的文件类型发送。浏览器会自行猜测，所以看起来一切正常；社交平台不猜，于是分享出去的链接没有配图。已修复，同时修正了缓存：手册的截图此前每次访问都要重新下载，现在由浏览器保留。站点还新增了 sitemap、robots 文件，以及可被机器读取的 /pricing.md 与 /llms.txt——当工作室主理人向 AI 助手询问该用什么系统时，它读的就是这些。服务 FAQ、服务条款、隐私政策与支持政策，现在各自拥有独立的中文网址，而不再是同一页面内的语言切换。

Media uploads repaired (v8.2.6)媒体上传修复（v8.2.6）

Every image upload — studio logo, home image, student photo, portfolio — was failing in production. Uploads work again, and a photo now costs the server about an eighth of the memory it did: a 24-megapixel file went from 139 MB of peak memory to 17 MB. Files beyond 30 megapixels are refused rather than allowed to exhaust the instance.

此前生产环境中所有图片上传（工作室 logo、首屏图、学员照片、作品集）均告失败。现已恢复，且服务器开销降至约八分之一：一张 2400 万像素的图片峰值内存由 139 MB 降至 17 MB。超过 3000 万像素的文件会被拒绝，而不是拖垮实例。

Operation log records everything (v8.2.3)操作日志完整记录（v8.2.3）

The studio operation log only ever showed check-ins and credit changes; archiving, renaming, scheduling and portfolio edits were performed but never listed. Every operator action is now recorded with the person who took it.

工作室操作日志此前只显示签到与课时变动，归档、改名、排课、作品集编辑虽已执行却从未列出。现在每一项操作都会连同操作人一并记录。

Theme colours resolved as a set (v8.2.7–v8.2.9)主题配色作为整体求解（v8.2.7–v8.2.9）

Three rounds of work on the eight studio themes. Large areas now stay in the brand's own colour family instead of being split by a second, near-opposite hue; and success, warning and danger are solved per theme so a warning badge can never end up looking like an ordinary button. All 45 status colours were re-derived against measured contrast targets.

对八套工作室主题进行了三轮处理。大面积区域不再被第二种近乎互补的色相劈开，而是保持在品牌自身的色系内；成功、警示、危险三色改为按主题求解，警示徽章不会再长得像普通按钮。全部 45 个状态色均按实测对比度目标重新推导。

Archiving and deleting a studio (v8.2.10)工作室归档与删除（v8.2.10）

Archiving a studio returned a server error and had never once succeeded in production, which also left permanent deletion unreachable. Both work, and each writes a full snapshot — database records, workspace and media — before anything is removed.

归档工作室此前返回服务器错误，在生产环境从未成功过，永久删除因此也无法进行。两者现均可用，且在移除任何内容之前都会写入完整快照：数据库记录、工作区与媒体文件。

Data retention (v8.2.19)数据保留（v8.2.19）

Audit records, analytics events, notification records and access sessions previously had no expiry. Retention now applies on a schedule — audit records are kept two years, analytics one year — so the service does not accumulate indefinitely. Consent records are deliberately exempt and are never deleted.

审计记录、分析事件、通知记录与访问会话此前没有任何有效期。现已按计划应用保留策略——审计记录保留两年、分析数据保留一年——服务不会无限累积。发布同意记录属于法律证据，明确豁免，永不删除。

The manual prints properly (v8.2.22–v8.2.23)手册的打印版已修正（v8.2.22–v8.2.23）

Printing the manual put body text on top of the page footer and produced 28 pages for what fits in 18. Both are fixed: nothing overprints, and a printed copy is a third shorter. The version and the licence are stated on the first page, and your browser's own print settings add the date and page numbers.

此前打印手册时，正文会压在页脚上，且 28 页的内容其实 18 页就装得下。两者均已修正：不再有文字重叠，打印件也短了三分之一。版本号与授权说明印在第一页，日期与页码由浏览器自身的打印设置提供。

User manual (v8.2.21)用户手册（v8.2.21）

A full user manual is now published at pwestudio.online/manual/ — how to get your website live, take enrolments, run the roster, handle credits and refunds, publish student work with consent, and what each role on your team can and cannot do. Chinese at /zh/manual/. It prints to a clean PDF, and every printed page carries the version and the date so an old copy says what it is.

完整的用户手册已发布在 pwestudio.online/zh/manual/ ——官网上线、招生报名、排课签到、课时与退费、作品公开授权，以及团队里每个角色能做什么、不能做什么。英文版在 /manual/。可直接打印为 PDF，每一页都带版本号与打印日期，旧的打印件会自己说清自己是什么。

English interface completed (v8.2.21)英文界面补全（v8.2.21）

Sixty-six strings in the operations CMS were still Chinese when the interface was set to English — most of them button labels read aloud by screen readers rather than text on screen. All are translated, and a check now runs over every screen so the gap cannot reopen quietly.

运营 CMS 在英文模式下仍有 66 处中文——其中多数是读屏软件会念出来、但屏幕上看不见的按钮说明。现已全部翻译，并新增了一项覆盖所有页面的检查，避免同样的缺口再次悄悄出现。

Pricing comes from the plan table (v8.2.19–v8.2.20)定价直接取自套餐表（v8.2.19–v8.2.20）

The public pricing page used to state its plan limits as fixed text, and they had already drifted from what the product enforces. The page now reads the plan table directly, so a figure it prints is a figure the system honours. A plan is also no longer published simply because it exists: publication is a setting an operator turns on, which keeps internal and test plans off the public page.

Four defects this deployment uncovered

本次部署发现的四个缺陷

All four looked fine from the outside and would otherwise have surfaced during an incident. They are listed because a release record that only lists successes is not evidence.

这四个缺陷从外部看都正常，若不排查将只会在事故中暴露。之所以列出，是因为只记录成功的发布记录不构成依据。

Daily backups had never succeeded每日备份从未成功过

The scheduled job called the backup script at the wrong path inside the image, and nothing read its output. Fixed, and the job now fails loudly.

定时任务调用了镜像内错误路径下的备份脚本，且无人读取其输出。已修复，任务失败现在会显式报错。

The dump could not be written备份文件无法写入

The backup directory’s ownership did not match the container’s user, so writes were denied. Ownership and mode are now asserted on every run, and an operator can still list backups without elevated rights.

备份目录归属与容器运行用户不匹配，写入被拒绝。现在每次运行都会校验归属与权限，运维人员也无需提权即可列出备份。

The restore rehearsal could never pass恢复演练不可能通过

An unpinned client package resolved to PostgreSQL 17 against a version 16 server, emitting a setting the server rejects. The client is now pinned to 16, and dumps produced by the newer client were deleted rather than left to fail mid-incident.

未固定版本的客户端包解析为 PostgreSQL 17，对 16 版服务端产生了其不接受的参数。客户端已固定为 16 版，由新版客户端产生的备份已删除，以免在事故中才发现不可用。

The media volume was empty媒体卷为空

The database referenced media assets and derivatives that the volume did not hold, so every brand logo returned a not-found. The media tree was restored, and the variant tool now verifies that a derivative file exists rather than only its database row.

数据库引用的媒体资源与派生文件在卷中并不存在，导致所有品牌 logo 返回未找到。媒体目录已恢复，派生工具现在校验派生文件本身是否存在，而不只是校验数据库记录。
