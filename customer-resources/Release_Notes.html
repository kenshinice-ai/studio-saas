Two corrections to v9.9.0 (v9.9.4)v9.9.0 的两处修正（v9.9.4）

The switch for Selected work said you had published nothing while your website was showing your pieces. The editor was asking the wrong question of its own records; the site was right all along, and the counter above the upload area was right too — which is how one screen managed to disagree with itself.

「工作室作品」的开关显示「还没有已发布的作品」，而你的官网正在展示作品。是编辑器向自己的记录问错了问题；网站一直是对的，上传区上方的计数也是对的——所以同一个界面才会自相矛盾。

A long navigation label was being shortened twice, once by the server and again by the browser, which left an ellipsis inside an ellipsis and a call-to-action button pressed against its own border. The server now decides alone, and the button — which has the least room and the most padding — is allowed less than the other entries.

过长的导航文字被截断了两次，服务端一次、浏览器又一次，于是出现省略号套省略号，行动按钮也顶到了自己的边框上。现在只由服务端决定；而那颗按钮空间最小、内边距最大，允许的字数也比其他条目更少。

Navigation that stays put, a name that follows you, and an address you can change (v9.9.0)导航不再跳走、店名跟着你走、网址可以更换（v9.9.0）

Clicking an entry in your site's navigation used to reload the whole page whenever the visitor arrived with anything after the address — a language choice, or the tracking tag on an advertisement. It scrolled instead of reloading now, and the visitor's language and the advertisement they came from both survive the click.

以前，只要访客带着地址后面的参数进站——选好的语言，或广告上的来源标记——点一下导航就会整页重新加载。现在是平滑滚动，访客的语言和他从哪个广告来的信息都不会丢。

Your four public pages now offer the same entries. Questions & Answers used to appear only in the home page's footer, and the timetable page linked to itself in a way the switch could not turn off.

四个公开页面现在提供同一组入口。以前「常见问题」只出现在首页页脚，课表页面还有一条自己关不掉的自链接。

Renaming your studio now reaches the places a machine reads: the browser tab, the card that appears when someone shares your link in WeChat or WhatsApp, and the name search engines show. Until now those kept whatever the studio was called on the day it was created, even though the page itself looked correct.

改店名现在会同步到机器读的那些地方：浏览器标签页、别人把你的链接分享到微信或 WhatsApp 时出现的卡片、搜索结果里的名字。在此之前，这些一直是工作室创建当天的旧名字——尽管页面本身看起来是对的。

Studio Admin says one thing one way. Every section switch reads Show X on the website, and Publish now belongs only to the button that puts your draft online; a switch labelled Publish that merely saved a draft was a fair thing to misread. All nine switches are in one list, each saying why a section you have turned on is not public yet — no student has agreed to show their work yet rather than a code. The principal has a panel of its own.

Studio Admin 里一件事只有一种说法。所有版块开关统一为「在官网上显示 XXX」，「发布」只留给真正把草稿推上线的那个按钮——一个叫「发布」却只是存草稿的开关，被误解并不奇怪。九个开关集中在一处，并且会说明你已经打开的版块为什么还没公开：显示「还没有学员同意公开作品」，而不是一串代码。主理人也有了自己的面板。

A studio can now change its public web address. The old address keeps working forever and redirects to the new one, so printed QR codes and flyers do not need reprinting; students, courses, work and schedules are untouched. Ask us to make the change — it is done once a year, from the platform side.

工作室现在可以更换公开网址。旧地址会永久保留并自动跳转到新地址，已经印出去的二维码和传单不需要重印；学员、课程、作品和排课完全不受影响。需要更换时告诉我们——每年一次，由平台侧操作。

The Chinese console is Chinese. Sixty-eight strings had no translation, including the sentence shown when a publish is still being confirmed.

中文后台是中文的。六十八条文案此前没有译文，其中包括发布仍在确认时显示的那一句。

Four defects this deployment uncovered

本次部署发现的四个缺陷

All four looked fine from the outside and would otherwise have surfaced during an incident. They are listed because a release record that only lists successes is not evidence.

这四个缺陷从外部看都正常，若不排查将只会在事故中暴露。之所以列出，是因为只记录成功的发布记录不构成依据。

Daily backups had never succeeded每日备份从未成功过

The scheduled job called the backup script at the wrong path inside the image, and nothing read its output. Fixed, and the job now fails loudly.

定时任务调用了镜像内错误路径下的备份脚本，且无人读取其输出。已修复，任务失败现在会显式报错。

The dump could not be written备份文件无法写入

The backup directory’s ownership did not match the container’s user, so writes were denied. Ownership and mode are now asserted on every run, and an operator can still list backups without elevated rights.

备份目录归属与容器运行用户不匹配，写入被拒绝。现在每次运行都会校验归属与权限，运维人员也无需提权即可列出备份。

The restore rehearsal could never pass恢复演练不可能通过

An unpinned client package resolved to PostgreSQL 17 against a version 16 server, emitting a setting the server rejects. The client is now pinned to 16, and dumps produced by the newer client were deleted rather than left to fail mid-incident.

未固定版本的客户端包解析为 PostgreSQL 17，对 16 版服务端产生了其不接受的参数。客户端已固定为 16 版，由新版客户端产生的备份已删除，以免在事故中才发现不可用。
