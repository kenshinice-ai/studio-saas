Hosting, data and recovery

托管、数据与恢复

Is this a production AWS deployment?这是正式的 AWS 生产部署吗？

Yes, since 30 July 2026. pwestudio.online runs from an AWS Lightsail instance in the Sydney region. The host terminates TLS with a Let’s Encrypt certificate covering the apex and www, renewed automatically; HTTP redirects to HTTPS and HSTS is set for one year. The application listens only on the instance’s loopback interface and is reached through that edge. The Cloudflare Tunnel used for earlier demonstrations is no longer the production path and will not be reintroduced for this hostname.

是，自 2026 年 7 月 30 日起。pwestudio.online 由位于悉尼区域的 AWS Lightsail 实例提供服务。主机使用 Let’s Encrypt 证书终止 TLS，覆盖主域与 www 并自动续期；HTTP 跳转至 HTTPS，HSTS 有效期一年。应用只监听实例本地回环地址，对外一律经该边缘转发。此前用于演示的 Cloudflare Tunnel 已不再是生产路径，本域名也不会再启用。

Where is our data stored?我们的数据存放在哪里？

In the Sydney region (ap-southeast-2), on the instance itself: PostgreSQL 16 runs beside the application, and media files sit on an attached volume. The runtime connects with a restricted database role; the owner role is used only for schema migrations at start-up. Managed services — Amazon RDS for the database, S3 for media, SES for mail — are not in use.

在悉尼区域（ap-southeast-2），且就在该实例上：PostgreSQL 16 与应用同机运行，媒体文件存放于挂载卷。运行时使用受限数据库角色连接；只有启动时的结构迁移才使用 owner 角色。托管服务尚未启用——数据库未用 Amazon RDS，媒体未用 S3，邮件未用 SES。

Are there backups, and has a restore actually been tested?有备份吗？恢复真的演练过吗？

Yes to both. A scheduled job runs daily at 03:15 UTC and produces a logical PostgreSQL dump plus an archive of the media volume. A restore rehearsal has been run against those artefacts and passes. Two limits are worth stating plainly: the copies currently live on the same instance, so an off-box copy is still an open item; and there is no automated alert if a backup run fails, so backup output is checked as an operator task rather than by monitoring.

两者都有。定时任务每日 UTC 03:15 执行，产出 PostgreSQL 逻辑备份与媒体卷归档。已针对这些产物完成恢复演练并通过。有两点需要明说：备份目前保存在同一实例上，异地副本仍是未完成项；备份失败没有自动告警，因此备份结果依赖运维人工检查，而非监控系统。

Is there monitoring and an availability commitment?有监控和可用性承诺吗？

Not yet. Uptime monitoring, backup-failure alerting, an on-call path and a contractual availability target are not in place. Health and version can be checked on demand, and a branded maintenance page is served if the application is briefly unavailable during an upgrade, but that is not the same as a monitored service with an SLA. Any availability target has to be written into a signed order form.

尚未完成。运行时监控、备份失败告警、值班响应路径与合同化可用性目标均未建立。可以按需查看健康状态与版本，升级期间应用短暂不可用时会展示品牌化维护页面，但这不等于「有监控、有 SLA 的服务」。任何可用性目标都必须写入已签署的订单。

Does PWE Studio require multi-factor authentication?PWE Studio 有多因素认证吗？

No, and this is now an open gap on a live service rather than a pre-launch to-do. Role separation, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing with 600,000 iterations, HttpOnly session cookies, rate limiting on student access codes and audit logging are all in place. Multi-factor authentication for privileged accounts is not, and remains our highest-priority security item. Until it ships, keep privileged logins individual, use a password manager, and tell us immediately if you suspect an account is compromised.

没有。这已经是「已上线服务上的公开缺口」，不再是上线前的待办事项。角色分离、PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256（600,000 次迭代）密码散列、HttpOnly 会话 Cookie、学员访问码速率限制与审计日志均已具备；特权账号的多因素认证尚未具备，是我们优先级最高的安全事项。在其上线前，请保持特权账号一人一号、使用密码管理器，并在怀疑账号被盗时立即通知我们。

Using the product

产品使用

What is the difference between Studio Admin and CMS?Studio Admin 与 CMS 有什么区别？

Studio Admin is the owner’s website and brand workspace. CMS is the daily operations workspace for enquiries, students, rosters, attendance, credits, artwork and reports. They share identity and stable links, while responsibilities and permissions remain separate.

Studio Admin 是主理人的网站与品牌工作台。CMS 是日常运营工作台，处理咨询、学员、排课、签到、课时、作品与报表。两者共用品牌识别与稳定入口，但职责与权限彼此独立。

Can families sign in?家长可以登录吗？

A family unlocks one student’s private area with the student name, the registered mobile number and a six-digit code issued by the studio. It shows that student’s credit balance, next class, attendance and portfolio, and nothing belonging to another student. Attempts are rate-limited and temporarily locked after repeated failures. Multi-child account aggregation is not part of v8.2.0.

家长使用学员姓名、登记手机号，以及工作室发放的六位访问码，解锁该名学员的私人区域。可见内容为该学员的课时余额、下次课、出勤与作品集，不含其他学员的任何信息。尝试次数受限，多次失败后会临时锁定。v8.2.0 不包含多子女账户汇总。

Does PWE Studio send SMS or email automatically?PWE Studio 会自动发送短信或邮件吗？

No. Communication actions open your own Mail or Messages application with prepared text, and you decide whether to send. There is no messaging provider, no delivery log, no bounce handling and no retry queue. Nothing is claimed about delivery.

不会。通信操作只会打开你设备自带的邮件或信息应用并预填文本，由你决定是否发送。系统未接入任何消息服务商，没有送达日志、退信处理或重试队列，也不对送达做任何承诺。

Are online payments included?包含在线支付吗？

No. Staff can record purchases, refunds and credit adjustments, and every one of those actions is audited. Card processing and automatic accounting reconciliation are deferred. Do not upload payment-card numbers into any field.

不包含。工作人员可以记录购买、退款与课时调整，且每一项都会写入审计记录。银行卡收款与自动对账属于延后项。请勿在任何字段中上传银行卡号。

Can any CSV or Excel file be imported?任何 CSV 或 Excel 文件都能导入吗？

We provide standard templates and a migration assessment. Arbitrary files usually need field mapping and may need paid clean-up. A production import only proceeds after an exceptions report, your written approval, a checkpoint, a rehearsal and a reconciliation.

我们提供标准模版与迁移评估。任意来源的文件通常需要字段映射，可能需要付费清洗。正式导入必须先完成异常报告、你的书面确认、检查点、演练与对账，才会执行。

What is in the ICS calendar export?ICS 日历导出包含什么？

There are two distinct exports. The weekly schedule contains active recurring classes, course details and location in the studio’s local time, and excludes student and guardian identities. The daily roster may contain student names because it is an operational attendance file; it is restricted to staff with data-export permission, is marked private before download and never contains guardian names.

系统提供两种不同的导出。周课表按工作室当地时间包含固定循环课程、课程信息与地点，不含学员或监护人身份。日名单属于签到运营文件，可能包含学员姓名；仅拥有数据导出权限的员工可下载，下载前明确标记为私密文件，且从不包含监护人姓名。

How are multiple campuses handled?多校区如何处理？

In v8.2.0 one campus is one tenant and one subscription. This keeps student records, staff membership, schedules, brand, plan and — importantly — backup, export, deletion and incident scope independent per campus. A future organisation layer may aggregate isolated campus tenants for reporting without merging their operational data.

v8.2.0 中，一个校区即一个租户、一份订阅。这样可以让学员记录、员工成员、排课、品牌、套餐，以及——同样重要的——备份、导出、删除与事故范围，按校区彼此独立。未来的组织层可能为报表聚合多个独立校区租户，但不会合并其运营数据。

Are custom domains and third-party integrations live?自定义域名与第三方集成上线了吗？

No. Your studio pages are served under the platform domain. Per-studio custom domains, Stripe, Xero and provider-driven calendar, email or SMS integrations are deferred unless a signed order specifically includes implementation and acceptance. Reserved interfaces are never described to you as working integrations.

没有。你的工作室页面目前托管在平台域名下。按工作室的自定义域名，以及 Stripe、Xero 和由服务商驱动的日历、邮件、短信集成，均属延后项，除非已签署订单明确包含实施与验收。预留接口不会被描述为可用集成。

Getting help. Read the support policy, then use Support & Feedback on the product home. Include your studio name, the affected role, the time and the page. Never put passwords, student access codes, payment details or sensitive student information in a first message.

需要帮助。请先阅读支持政策，再使用产品首页的「支持与反馈」。请提供工作室名称、涉及角色、时间与页面。首次沟通中请勿包含密码、学员访问码、付款信息或敏感学员资料。
