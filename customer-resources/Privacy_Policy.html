Privacy policy

隐私政策

This is a draft, not legal advice. It describes how PWE Studio actually behaves today, in plain English, so that it can be reviewed against reality. It has not been reviewed by an Australian lawyer. Where it and a signed service agreement differ, the signed agreement prevails. Two sections in particular — consent for children and retention and deletion — should be reviewed by an Australian legal practitioner before this page is relied on commercially.

本文为草案，不构成法律意见。它以平实语言描述 PWE Studio 目前的实际行为，便于与事实逐条核对。本文尚未经澳大利亚律师复核。若本文与已签署的服务协议不一致，以签署协议为准。其中两节——儿童相关同意与数据留存与删除——建议在正式商业依赖前由澳大利亚执业律师复核。

2 · Who is responsible for what

二 · 责任如何划分

This matters more than it sounds, because PWE Studio is sold in two forms and the answer differs.

这一点比听起来更重要，因为 PWE Studio 有两种交付形态，责任归属并不相同。

Subscription (SaaS)订阅（SaaS）

The studio decides what student and family information to collect and why, and holds the direct relationship with those families. We host and process that information in order to provide, secure and support the service. We do not sell it, mine it, or use it for our own marketing.

由工作室决定收集哪些学员与家庭信息及其目的，并与这些家庭保持直接关系。我们托管并处理这些信息，用于提供、保护与支持服务。我们不出售、不挖掘，也不用于我方自身营销。

Edition (self-hosted buy-out)独立版（买断自持）

The studio runs the software on its own infrastructure. Student and family information never reaches us, and we hold no copy of it. The studio is responsible for its own hosting, backups, access control and breach response; we can only advise.

工作室在自有基础设施上运行本软件。学员与家庭信息不会到达我方，我方也不持有任何副本。托管、备份、访问控制与事故响应由工作室自行负责，我方仅能提供建议。

Separately from either form, we hold a small amount of information about the studio as our customer: contact names, business email and phone, billing details and support correspondence. That is our own record and this policy governs it directly.

除上述两种形态外，我方另会保存少量关于工作室作为我方客户的信息：联系人姓名、业务邮箱与电话、账务信息与支持往来记录。这属于我方自有记录，直接受本政策约束。

4 · Children’s information and publication consent

四 · 儿童信息与作品公开同意

Most students at a creative studio are children, so this is the part of the product designed most conservatively.

创意类工作室的学员多为儿童，因此这是产品中设计最为保守的部分。

Private by default

默认私有

Every photograph of student work is private when it is uploaded. It is visible to studio staff with the appropriate role, and to the family that holds that student’s access code — nobody else. Original files are never served publicly; public and family views are generated derivatives, and every request is checked against the owning studio.

学员作品照片在上传时即为私有。可见范围限于具备相应角色的工作室员工，以及持有该学员访问码的家庭，此外无人可见。原始文件从不公开提供；公开视图与家庭视图使用生成的派生文件，且每次请求都会校验所属工作室。

Who can see one student’s records

谁能看到某位学员的记录

A family unlocks the student area with the student’s name, the registered mobile number and a six-digit code the studio issues. That combination reveals that one student’s credit balance, next class, attendance and work — and nothing about any other student. Attempts are rate-limited and locked temporarily after repeated failures. The weekly schedule export excludes student and guardian identities. A separate daily roster export may contain student names because it is an operational attendance file; it is restricted to staff with data-export permission, is marked private before download and never contains guardian names.

家庭使用学员姓名、登记手机号与工作室发放的六位访问码解锁学员专区。该组合只显示这一名学员的课时余额、下次课、出勤与作品，不包含任何其他学员的信息。尝试次数受限，多次失败后临时锁定。周课表导出不含学员或监护人身份。另有一份日名单用于签到运营，可能包含学员姓名；仅拥有数据导出权限的员工可下载，下载前明确标记为私密文件，且从不包含监护人姓名。

Publication requires a separate, recorded decision

公开展示需要单独且留痕的决定

Showing a student’s work on a studio’s public website or in promotional material is a distinct decision, never implied by enrolment. The registration form has a separate optional consent; ticking it requires naming the consenting person and their relationship to the student — the student themselves if an adult, a parent, or another authorised guardian. Each consent event is stored with who gave it, the relationship claimed, the method and the time, so a studio can later show what it was authorised to publish.

将学员作品展示在工作室公开网站或宣传材料上，是一项独立决定，绝不因报名而被默认。报名表中设有单独的可选同意项；勾选时必须填写授权人姓名及其与学员的关系——成年学员本人、家长，或其他获授权的监护人。每次同意事件都会记录授权人、所声明的关系、方式与时间，便于工作室日后证明其被授权公开的范围。

Withdrawing consent

撤回同意

Consent can be withdrawn at any time, without giving a reason, by contacting the studio. Withdrawal is recorded as its own event beside the original consent, and the work returns to private. Two honest limits: material a studio has already printed, or that a third party has already copied elsewhere, cannot be recalled by us; and withdrawal removes public display, it does not delete the teaching record, which is handled under section 7.

同意可随时撤回，无需说明理由，联系工作室即可。撤回会作为独立事件记录在原同意旁，作品恢复为私有。有两点需要如实说明：工作室已经印制的材料，或第三方已在别处复制的内容，我方无法收回；撤回只取消公开展示，并不删除教学记录，后者适用第七节。

The studio, not us, is responsible for confirming that the person giving consent actually has authority for that child. The product records the claim; it cannot verify a family relationship.

确认作出同意者是否确实对该儿童具有授权，是工作室的责任，而非我方。产品记录该项声明，但无法核实亲属关系。

5 · Where information is held

五 · 信息保存位置

For subscription customers, data is held in Amazon Web Services’ Sydney region (ap-southeast-2), on the instance that runs the service: PostgreSQL beside the application, and media files on an attached volume. Backups are produced daily and are currently stored on the same instance; an off-instance copy is an open item we disclose rather than imply. Transport is encrypted with TLS, HTTP requests are redirected to HTTPS, and the application is not directly reachable from the internet.

订阅客户的数据保存在 Amazon Web Services 悉尼区域（ap-southeast-2），位于运行本服务的实例上：PostgreSQL 与应用同机，媒体文件存放于挂载卷。备份每日生成，目前保存在同一实例上；异地副本是我方主动披露的未完成项，而非暗示已具备。传输使用 TLS 加密，HTTP 请求跳转至 HTTPS，应用不可从互联网直接访问。

We do not transfer personal information overseas for our own purposes. Support correspondence you send us may pass through the email provider you chose to use.

我方不会为自身目的将个人信息传输至境外。你发送给我方的支持往来邮件，可能经由你自己选用的邮件服务商传输。

6 · How information is protected, and what is still missing

六 · 如何保护，以及仍缺什么

In place已具备

Each studio’s data is scoped to its own tenant and every authenticated route carries that context. Staff roles are separated — owner, manager, teacher, front desk — and granted least privilege. Passwords are stored as PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hashes with a per-password salt and 600,000 iterations. Sessions use HttpOnly cookies with same-site controls. Student access attempts are rate-limited and locked. Material administrative actions are audited. Original media is private and access is ownership-checked. Deleting a studio requires an explicit lifecycle state and confirmation.

每个工作室的数据限定在其自身租户内，所有需认证的路由都携带该上下文。员工角色分离——主理人、管理者、教师、前台——并按最小权限授予。密码以 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 存储，每条密码独立加盐，迭代 600,000 次。会话使用 HttpOnly Cookie 并设置同站策略。学员访问尝试受速率限制并会锁定。重要管理操作写入审计。原始媒体私有，访问需校验归属。删除工作室需要明确的生命周期状态与确认。

Open gaps we disclose我方披露的缺口

Multi-factor authentication is not yet enforced for privileged accounts; this is our highest-priority security item. Backup copies currently live on the same instance as the service. There is no uptime or backup-failure monitoring and no on-call roster, so failures are found by operator checks rather than alerts. There is no contractual availability commitment unless a signed order form states one.

特权账号尚未强制多因素认证，这是我方优先级最高的安全事项。备份副本目前与服务位于同一实例。没有运行监控与备份失败告警，也没有值班名单，因此故障依靠运维检查发现，而非自动告警。除已签署订单另有约定外，不存在合同化可用性承诺。

We list the gaps because a privacy policy that only lists controls is a sales document. The Australian Cyber Security Centre treats multi-factor authentication, patching and tested backups as core measures for a small business; we treat MFA and an off-instance backup copy as work owed, not optional polish.

我们列出缺口，因为只列举控制措施的隐私政策其实是销售材料。澳大利亚网络安全中心将多因素认证、补丁更新与经演练的备份视为小企业的基础措施；我方将 MFA 与异地备份副本视为应偿还的工作，而非可选的锦上添花。

7 · Retention and deletion

七 · 留存与删除

While a studio is a customer, its records are kept for as long as it needs them to run classes and account for credits. The studio decides what to keep and what to remove, and can correct or delete a student record at any time from within the product.

在工作室仍为客户期间，其记录会保留至其运营课程与核对课时所需的期限。保留与删除由工作室决定，并可随时在产品内更正或删除某条学员记录。

After a subscription ends we make a standard export available for a limited window, then delete or de-identify the data according to the agreed deletion schedule, except where a law requires longer retention. Backup expiry takes longer than live deletion because a backup is a point-in-time copy; the exact figures belong in the production backup schedule and the signed agreement rather than on this page.

订阅结束后，我方会在限定期限内提供标准导出，随后按约定的删除计划删除或去标识化，除法律要求更长留存的情形。备份的失效时间长于线上删除，因为备份是某一时点的副本；具体期限应写入生产备份计划与签署协议，而不写在本页。

Needs legal review. Retention periods for children’s teaching records, and the interaction between deletion requests and record-keeping obligations, must be set with Australian legal advice before this section is treated as final.

需律师复核。儿童教学记录的留存期限，以及删除请求与记录保存义务之间的关系，须在获得澳大利亚法律意见后确定，本节方可视为定稿。

八 · 查阅、更正与投诉

If you are a student or family member, ask your studio first — it holds the relationship and can act on the record directly. If a studio cannot resolve it, or if your question is about information we hold as the software supplier, email lee.liu.melbourne@gmail.com. We will acknowledge the request, confirm who you are by a means that does not create a new risk, and respond within a reasonable period.

若你是学员或家庭成员，请先联系你的工作室——关系由其持有，且可直接处理相应记录。若工作室无法解决，或你的问题涉及我方作为软件供应商所持有的信息，请发送邮件至 lee.liu.melbourne@gmail.com。我方会确认收到请求，以不产生新风险的方式核实你的身份，并在合理期限内答复。

We do not publish a response deadline here. Support response targets exist only where a signed order form states them, and they are initial-response targets rather than resolution guarantees. A request under privacy law is answered within the period that law requires, which is not something we shorten by writing a number on this page.

本页不设具体答复时限。支持响应目标仅在已签署订单载明时存在，且属首次响应目标，而非解决时限承诺。依隐私法提出的请求，将在该法律要求的期限内答复；在本页另写一个数字并不会缩短它。

We publish a location rather than a street address, so email is currently the channel for written requests. If you need to correspond by post, ask us and we will supply a postal address for that request.

我方目前公布所在地而非街道地址，因此书面请求请通过电子邮件提出。若你确需以邮寄方式往来，请与我方联系，我方将为该请求提供通信地址。

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. We would rather hear from you first, but that path is yours regardless.

若你对我方答复不满，可向澳大利亚信息专员办公室（OAIC）投诉。我方希望先有机会处理，但该途径始终属于你的权利。

9 · If something goes wrong

九 · 发生问题时

On a suspected data incident we contain access and preserve evidence; identify which studios, people and data types are affected; rotate exposed credentials and revoke sessions; assess likely harm; notify the affected studio’s contact without unreasonable delay; agree who leads any notification to families and to the regulator; restore only from verified evidence; and write up cause, decisions and follow-up tests.

在怀疑发生数据事故时，我方会：控制访问并保全证据；确认受影响的工作室、人员与数据类型；轮换已暴露的凭据并注销会话；评估可能造成的损害；不无故延迟地通知受影响工作室的联系人；商定由谁主导向家庭与监管机构的通知；仅基于已核验的证据进行恢复；并记录原因、决策与后续验证。

Australia’s Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires covered entities to notify affected individuals and the Commissioner where a breach is likely to cause serious harm. Which entity is covered, and who notifies, must be assessed at the time rather than assumed — the studio usually has the direct relationship with the families.

澳大利亚「可通报数据泄露」（NDB）制度要求受约束实体在泄露可能造成严重损害时，通知受影响个人及专员。哪一方受约束、由谁通知，须在事发时评估而非预先假定——通常是工作室与家庭之间存在直接关系。

Breach notifications and security reports reach us at lee.liu.melbourne@gmail.com, attention Lee L, Privacy Contact. If you believe student information has been exposed, say so in the subject line and do not include the exposed data itself in the message.

数据泄露通知与安全报告请发送至 lee.liu.melbourne@gmail.com，收件人 Lee L（隐私事务联系人）。若你认为学员信息已经外泄，请在邮件主题中说明，并不要在邮件正文中附上外泄的数据本身。

产品内的收集说明带有版本号，家庭的同意会与其所见版本一并记录。若本政策的变更会实质影响信息处理方式，我方将在变更生效前书面告知订阅中的工作室。
