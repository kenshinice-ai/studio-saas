What v8.1.0 changes

v8.1.0 的改动

The previous release was accepted for local demonstration only. This one takes the service into production, then fixes what going live and a full readability review exposed. Nothing below is a plan; each item is already in the release.

上一版仅按本地演示验收。本版将服务带入生产，并修复上线与一次完整可读性检查所暴露的问题。以下均非计划，全部已包含在本次发布中。

Production hosting生产托管

The service moved to a dedicated AWS instance on 30 July 2026 with its own certificate, daily database backups and a rehearsed restore. The tunnel used for earlier invitation demonstrations is no longer part of the production path.

服务于 2026 年 7 月 30 日迁至专属 AWS 实例，具备独立证书、每日数据库备份与已演练的恢复流程。此前用于邀请演示的隧道不再属于生产链路。

Registration confirmation is readable again报名成功提示恢复可读

On the seven dark studio themes, the card a parent sees after submitting a registration painted fixed light text on what the theme had turned into a light surface — measured at 1.06:1, effectively invisible. It now takes both colours from the theme, a pair the palette generator already checks at 4.5:1 or better across all fifteen theme variants.

在七个深色工作室主题下，家长提交报名后看到的成功卡使用了固定浅色文字，而主题已把底色变浅——实测 1.06:1，几乎不可见。现在两种颜色都取自主题，而这一组颜色由调色生成器在全部十五个主题变体上校验，均不低于 4.5:1。

Your branding now reaches the CMS品牌配色贯通到 CMS

The daily operations workspace applied only part of a studio's chosen theme and then painted a fixed grey background over it, so every studio's CMS looked the same. The public portal, the registration page and the CMS now apply one identical, complete theme set, with a test asserting the three agree field for field.

日常运营工作台此前只应用了工作室所选主题的一部分，又以固定灰色背景覆盖其上，导致所有工作室的 CMS 外观雷同。现在公开门户、报名页与 CMS 应用同一套完整主题，并有测试逐字段断言三者一致。

Focus and form outlines meet the standard焦点与表单描边达标

The keyboard focus ring on the product homepage measured 1.70:1 against the page, below the 3:1 a non-text indicator requires; it is now 4.52:1, and the brighter identity amber is kept where it is correct at 9.70:1. Form field outlines on dark sections moved from 2.51:1 to 3.90:1.

产品主页的键盘焦点环对页面底色实测 1.70:1，低于非文本指示所需的 3:1；现为 4.52:1，而在合适的深色区块仍保留 9.70:1 的亮琥珀。深色区块的表单描边由 2.51:1 提升至 3.90:1。

Plan allowances revised套餐额度调整

Monthly prices are unchanged. Starter now includes 1 team account and 2 GB; Studio 5 accounts and 10 GB; Growth 1,000 students and 50 GB, keeping its 20 team accounts. Exceeding an allowance refuses the next addition — it never deletes existing students, accounts or media.

月费不变。Starter 现含 1 个团队账号与 2 GB；Studio 含 5 个账号与 10 GB；Growth 含 1,000 名学员与 50 GB，团队账号仍为 20 个。超出额度只会拒绝新增，绝不会删除既有学员、账号或媒体。

Published policies已发布的政策文件

A privacy policy and terms of service are now published rather than promised, naming the operating entity and its ABN. Both are drafts pending Australian legal review and say so on the page.

隐私政策与服务条款已实际发布而非仅作承诺，并载明运营主体与 ABN。两者均为待澳大利亚律师复核的草案，页面上已明确标注。

Four defects this deployment uncovered

本次部署发现的四个缺陷

All four looked fine from the outside and would otherwise have surfaced during an incident. They are listed because a release record that only lists successes is not evidence.

这四个缺陷从外部看都正常，若不排查将只会在事故中暴露。之所以列出，是因为只记录成功的发布记录不构成依据。

Daily backups had never succeeded每日备份从未成功过

The scheduled job called the backup script at the wrong path inside the image, and nothing read its output. Fixed, and the job now fails loudly.

定时任务调用了镜像内错误路径下的备份脚本，且无人读取其输出。已修复，任务失败现在会显式报错。

The dump could not be written备份文件无法写入

The backup directory’s ownership did not match the container’s user, so writes were denied. Ownership and mode are now asserted on every run, and an operator can still list backups without elevated rights.

备份目录归属与容器运行用户不匹配，写入被拒绝。现在每次运行都会校验归属与权限，运维人员也无需提权即可列出备份。

The restore rehearsal could never pass恢复演练不可能通过

An unpinned client package resolved to PostgreSQL 17 against a version 16 server, emitting a setting the server rejects. The client is now pinned to 16, and dumps produced by the newer client were deleted rather than left to fail mid-incident.

未固定版本的客户端包解析为 PostgreSQL 17，对 16 版服务端产生了其不接受的参数。客户端已固定为 16 版，由新版客户端产生的备份已删除，以免在事故中才发现不可用。

The media volume was empty媒体卷为空

The database referenced media assets and derivatives that the volume did not hold, so every brand logo returned a not-found. The media tree was restored, and the variant tool now verifies that a derivative file exists rather than only its database row.

数据库引用的媒体资源与派生文件在卷中并不存在，导致所有品牌 logo 返回未找到。媒体目录已恢复，派生工具现在校验派生文件本身是否存在，而不只是校验数据库记录。

Still deferred仍未完成

What we do not claim

我们不作承诺的部分

  • Multi-factor authentication for privileged accounts. Now an open gap on a live service, and our highest-priority security item.
  • 特权账号的多因素认证。这已是已上线服务上的公开缺口，也是我们优先级最高的安全事项。
  • Off-instance backup copies. Backups exist and restore correctly, but currently live on the same instance.
  • 异地备份副本。备份已存在且可正确恢复，但目前保存在同一实例上。
  • Uptime monitoring, backup-failure alerting, on-call ownership and a contractual SLA.
  • 运行监控、备份失败告警、值班归属与合同化 SLA。
  • Managed AWS services. Amazon RDS, S3 and SES are not in use.
  • AWS 托管服务。尚未使用 Amazon RDS、S3 与 SES。
  • Automated messaging. Mail and Messages open on the user’s device; no provider delivery, logs or retries.
  • 自动化消息发送。仅在用户设备上打开邮件或信息应用；无服务商送达、日志与重试。
  • Online payments and accounting sync.
  • 在线支付与自动对账。
  • Per-studio custom domains.
  • 按工作室的自定义域名。
  • Organisation-level multi-campus aggregation. One campus remains one tenant.
  • 组织级多校区汇总。目前仍是一个校区一个租户。
  • Two remaining readability items inside the operations workspace. The CMS still carries a second, older dark-appearance mechanism alongside the theme one, and a large number of its secondary labels are still lighter than the contrast standard asks. Both are recorded with an owner and a fix, and neither affects what a parent or student sees.
  • 运营工作台内仍有两项可读性问题。CMS 中除主题机制外仍保留一套较早的深色实现；其大量次要标签的对比度仍低于标准要求。两项均已登记责任人与修复方案，且都不影响家长与学员所见界面。

A final privacy policy, service agreement and data-processing schedule still require Australian legal review. The published privacy policy and terms of service are drafts that describe actual product behaviour; they are not legal advice.

最终版隐私政策、服务协议与数据处理附表仍需澳大利亚律师复核。已发布的隐私政策与服务条款为草案，如实描述产品实际行为，但不构成法律意见。
