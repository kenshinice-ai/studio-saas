Support policy

支持政策

How to reach us, what to include, and how support is prioritised. Contractual support hours and targets exist only when a signed order form states them.

如何联系我们、首次沟通应提供什么，以及支持如何分级。支持时间与响应目标只有在已签署订单载明时才构成合同承诺。

Current contact path. Use Support & Feedback on the PWE Studio homepage. It opens your device's Mail or Messages application with prepared text; you review and send it. PWE Studio does not claim automated delivery, ticket tracking or 24/7 monitoring.

当前联系路径。请使用 PWE Studio 首页的「支持与反馈」。该功能会打开设备自带的邮件或信息应用并预填文字，由你检查后发送。PWE Studio 不宣称具备自动送达、工单追踪或全天候监控。

Never include passwords, access codes, payment details or sensitive student information in the first message. Include the studio name, affected role, approximate time, page and a non-sensitive description.

首次沟通中请勿包含密码、访问码、付款信息或敏感学员资料。请提供工作室名称、涉及角色、大致时间、页面与不含敏感信息的问题描述。

What happens after contact

联系之后

  1. We acknowledge the issue and confirm its priority.我方确认收到问题并确定优先级。
  2. We request the minimum reproducible, non-sensitive evidence.我方索取最少且不含敏感信息的复现依据。
  3. Security and data risks are contained before ordinary diagnosis.安全与数据风险优先于一般诊断进行控制。
  4. We identify the current version, delivery mode, tenant and affected workflow.我方确认当前版本、交付形态、租户与受影响流程。
  5. We communicate a safe workaround or the next update, then verify any repair in the affected surface.我方提供安全替代方案或下次更新时间，并在受影响界面验证修复。
