00 · Before you start开始之前

Five screens, and three ideas that explain the rest

五个界面，和三个先弄懂就不会错的概念

Your studio has its own address on the platform — a short URL name we call the slug, fixed when the studio is created. Everything below hangs off it.

你的工作室在平台上有一个自己的网址标识（slug），开通时确定、之后不可更改。下面所有地址都挂在它下面。

Screen界面 Address地址 Who uses it给谁用
Portal 官网门户 /<slug> Visitors and families — courses, work, enquiry form, student area 访客与家长——课程、作品、报名表、学员专区
Register 快速报名 /<slug>/register A standalone enquiry page you can put behind a QR code 独立报名页，可放在二维码后面直接转发
CMS 运营后台 /<slug>/cms Your team — students, roster, check-in, credits, work, approvals 你的团队——学员、排课、签到、课时、作品、审批
Studio Admin 品牌工作台 /<slug>/studio-admin The owner only — brand, theme, website content, enquiry form 仅限 Owner——品牌、主题、官网内容、报名表

Three ideas worth five minutes now

三个概念，现在花五分钟

1 · A credit is not a class

1 · 课时不等于「一节课」

A credit is a prepaid unit of teaching time. A long class can consume more than one, which is why families are shown a credit balance rather than “classes left”. Credits are bought by top-up, deducted at check-in, and refundable. A “package” bundles credits for sale; the word plan is reserved for your subscription tier with us, and only the platform can change that.

课时是预付的教学时间单位。一节长课可能扣不止一个课时，所以给家长显示的是「剩余课时」而不是「还能上几节课」。课时通过充值购入、签到扣减、可退。「课包」是课时的售卖组合；「套餐」一词专指你与平台之间的订阅档位，只有平台方能改。

2 · Families do not have accounts

2 · 家长没有账号

There is no parent login. A family checks their own records with student name + registered phone + a six-digit access code your team hands them. The code is shown in plain text once, at the moment it is created, and never stored in a readable form. See section 06.

系统没有家长登录。家庭用学员姓名 + 登记手机号 + 6 位访问码自助查询，访问码由你的团队生成后交给家长。明文只在生成时显示一次，系统只保存哈希。见第 06 章。

3 · Draft is not published

3 · 草稿不等于已发布

In Studio Admin, Save Draft changes nothing the public can see. Only Publish updates the portal, the register page and the CMS — and every publish keeps a version you can roll back to.

在品牌工作台里，Save Draft 不会改变任何公开可见的内容，只有 Publish 才会更新门户、报名页和 CMS——而且每次发布都留一个可回滚的版本。

Worth knowing先说清楚

Course names, work titles, addresses, phone numbers and people’s names are never translated. They are shown exactly as your team typed them, in either language. Only the interface and the section headings switch. This is a deliberate decision, not a gap: a course called “周末水彩班” is that course’s name, and a machine rendering of it would be a different course.

课程名、作品标题、地址、电话和人名永远不翻译，两种语言下都按录入原样显示，只有界面文字与板块标题会切换。这是产品决策而非缺陷：叫「周末水彩班」的课程，它的名字就是这个；机器翻一版出来就成了另一门课。

01 · Day one第一天

Put your website up before you put anyone in it

先把官网立起来，再往里放人

Everything on this screen is the owner’s. A manager who opens it is told to use the CMS instead — brand and public content are an owner boundary, enforced by the server rather than hidden in the interface.

这一屏的所有内容都属于 Owner。Manager 打开会被提示改用 CMS——品牌与公开内容是 Owner 的边界，由服务端强制，不是界面上藏起来。

  1. Open /<slug>/studio-admin and sign in with the email and temporary password the platform gave you. Change the password immediately (minimum 8 characters).
  2. 打开 /<slug>/studio-admin，用平台给你的邮箱和临时密码登录，立刻改掉初始密码（至少 8 位）。
  3. Pick an industry preset. It fills slogan, hero copy, enquiry questions, FAQ and a recommended theme in one go — and it overwrites what is there. Do it first, before you write anything. There is an Undo bar if you regret it.
  4. 选行业预设。它会一次性填好 slogan、首屏文案、报名问题、FAQ 和推荐主题——并且会覆盖已有内容。所以要最先做，写过东西之后再点就晚了。点错了顶部有 Undo 条。
  5. Choose a theme. Eight palettes, each designed in light and dark. Arcade Lime is dark only; a mode a theme does not offer is greyed out rather than approximated.
  6. 选配色主题。八套主题，每套都有明暗两版设计；街机青柠仅暗色，某套主题不提供的模式会置灰，而不是凑一个出来。
  7. Fill in the studio details, hero, sections and enquiry questions. A section with no content stays hidden even when its switch is on, so an unfinished page never shows an empty shell.
  8. 填工作室信息、首屏、板块与报名问题。没写内容的板块即使开关打开也不会显示——没写完的页面不会露出空壳。
  9. Save Draft, preview, then Publish. The preview switches between the portal and the register page, both languages, desktop and mobile.
  10. 存草稿、预览、再发布。预览区可切换门户 / 报名页、中英文、桌面 / 手机。

Publishing is gated on purpose

发布有质量门槛，是故意的

Publish runs three checks and will stop you: the studio name must be filled; the public essentials must be complete (hero title, enquiry title and intro, a phone number or email, and the principal’s name if that section is on — placeholder text is rejected too); and six contrast pairs across body text, secondary text and button labels must each reach 4.5:1.

Publish 会跑三道检查并拦住你：工作室名称必填；公开内容完整（首屏标题、报名标题与引导语、电话或邮箱、开启主理人板块时的主理人姓名——占位文案同样会被拒）；正文、次要文字与按钮文字共六项对比度必须各自达到 4.5:1。

The one people hit最常踩的一个

“I saved but the site didn’t change.” Save Draft is not Publish. And if Publish is blocked by Improve colour contrast before publishing, you have fine-tuned a colour below 4.5:1 — go back to the recommended theme, or keep adjusting until the warning clears. The gate is there because an unreadable page is worse than an unbranded one.

「我保存了，网站怎么没变？」——Save Draft 不是 Publish。如果 Publish 被 Improve colour contrast before publishing 拦住，是你微调的某个颜色掉到 4.5:1 以下了：回到推荐主题，或继续调到警告消失。这道门存在的理由是：看不清的页面比没做品牌的页面更糟。

Status colours follow the theme

状态色会跟着主题变

The theme preview shows two groups: six theme colours (page, panel, accent, support, control boundary, focus ring) and three status colours (success, warning, danger) marked tuned to this theme. Those three are solved per theme — 45 values in total across the eight themes and their modes — so success and warning stay readable and stay distinguishable from your accent. Changing theme changes them. That is correct, not a bug.

主题预览分两组：六个主题色（页面、面板、强调色、辅助色、控件边界、聚焦环）与三个状态色（成功、警示、危险），后者标注「已按本主题调校」。这三个颜色是按主题逐一求解的——八套主题及其明暗模式合计 45 个值——保证既看得清，又不会和你的强调色撞脸。换主题它们会跟着变，这是正确行为。

02 · Enrolment招生与报名

From an enquiry to a student record

从一条咨询，到一份学员档案

Families submit from the portal or the standalone register page — same form, same queue. Both feed the CMS Pending tab, which carries a red count badge in the navigation.

家长可以从门户或独立报名页提交——同一张表、同一个队列，都进入 CMS 的待审核标签页，导航上带红色数字角标。

Each card shows name, contact, birthday, message, submission time, source (portal or quick register) and a status: pending, contacted, trial booked, following up, approved, enrolled, rejected, duplicate, lost, archived.

每张卡片显示姓名、联系方式、生日、留言、提交时间、来源（门户网站 / 快速报名）与状态：待审核、已联系、已约试听、跟进中、已批准、已建档、已拒绝、重复申请、已流失、已归档。

Approving creates the record

批准即建档

Enter a starting credit balance (zero is fine) and press Approve. The student record is created and the credits are posted in one step. Rejecting simply removes the card from the list.

填入初始课时数（可以是 0），点批准建档，系统一步创建学员档案并把课时入账。点「拒绝」则该申请不再显示。

The amber “possible duplicate” badge琥珀色的「疑似重复」角标

When two pending applications share a phone number, both cards are flagged. Do not approve both. Call the family first: a resubmission means approve the fuller one and reject the other; two children on one parent’s number means approve both, which is normal. Approving blindly creates two student records, and from then on the credit ledger is split across them. A separate warning, this phone already has a student, means the number matches an existing record.

两条待审核申请共用同一手机号时，两张卡片都会被标记。不要两条都批。先给家长打个电话：重复提交就保留信息最全的一条、拒绝另一条；两个孩子共用家长号码属于正常，分别批准即可。盲批会建出两份学员档案，此后课时账就一分为二了。另有一条提示「此电话已有学员」，表示该号码与已建档学员重复。

What the family consented to is recorded

家长同意了什么，会被记录下来

The enquiry form requires a privacy consent tick, and the consent is stored together with the version number of the privacy notice in force at that moment. Publication consent for student work is optional at this stage and recorded the same way. Both are append-only: a withdrawal is a new event, never an edit to the old one.

报名表必须勾选隐私同意，系统会把当时生效的隐私声明版本号与同意记录一起存档；作品公开授权在这一步是选填的，记录方式相同。两者都是只增不改：撤回是一条新事件，而不是把旧记录改掉。

03 · Every day每一天

The roster, and the two minutes at the start of class

排课，和上课前的两分钟

Weekly classes are set up once — name, weekday, start time, length, capacity, students — and appear on that day’s list automatically from then on. You can also drop a student into a single day by hand. The default start time for new manual entries is a studio setting; changing it does not touch anything already saved.

固定班次只需设一次——名称、周几、开始时间、时长、容量、学员——之后每周自动出现在当日名单里；也可以把某个学员手动加进某一天。新建手动排课的默认上课时间是工作室级设置，改它不会动到已经保存的排课。

  1. Open Daily roster. It opens on today.
  2. 打开每日排课，默认就是今天。
  3. Check students in as they arrive. Each check-in deducts the credits for that class and writes a ledger entry. Batch check-in skips anyone already checked in or out of credits, and says so.
  4. 到场即签到。每次签到扣减对应课时并写入账本；批量签到会自动跳过已签到或余额为 0 的学员并给出提示。
  5. Checked in the wrong person? Reverse it. The credits go back, the attendance is marked reversed, and the ledger keeps a matching correcting entry. Nothing is erased.
  6. 签错人了就撤销。课时退回，考勤标记为已作废，账本留下对应的冲正流水——历史不会被抹掉。

Two calendar exports, and they are not the same file两个日历导出，不是同一个文件

Weekly timetable ICS contains recurring classes and no student names — that is the one you can share. Today’s roster ICS contains the names of the students attending; it is an internal operating file. Check which button you pressed before you forward it.

固定课表 ICS 只含周期班次、不含学员姓名，这个可以对外分享。当日 ICS 含当天到课学员的姓名，是内部运营文件。转发之前先确认你点的是哪一个。

A student with no credits left is stopped at check-in, with a prompt — not discovered at the end of the month. Birthdays within the next 14 days show as a banner, and one tap copies a birthday message with the student’s and studio’s name already filled in.

余额不足的学员会在签到当场被拦下并提示，而不是到月底才发现。未来 14 天内过生日的学员会显示横幅，点一下即可复制已经填好学员名和工作室名的生日祝福。

04 · Money钱

The ledger is the balance. Nobody types over it.

余额就是账本本身，没有人能直接改它

A student’s balance is the sum of its entries, not a number stored somewhere and edited. Every movement records who did it, when, and why. That is what makes “how many classes are left?” answerable without anyone’s memory being involved.

学员余额是每一笔流水之和，不是存在某处、可以被改掉的数字。每次变动都记录操作人、时间与原因——这就是为什么「还剩几节课」这个问题不需要靠任何人的记忆来回答。

Taking a payment

收费

  1. Search and select the student. Their photo, current balance and last three movements appear — check them before you touch anything.
  2. 搜索并选中学员。会显示头像、当前余额和最近 3 笔流水——动手前先核对。
  3. Pick a package to fill in credits and amount, or type both by hand.
  4. 点「套餐快选」自动填入课时数与金额，或手动填写两者。
  5. Choose the payment method, add a note if useful, and confirm. A ready-to-send confirmation message can be copied in one tap.
  6. 选付款方式、按需填备注、确认收款入账。成功后可一键复制发给家长的确认话术。

Refunds are a different permission

退费是另一个权限

The refund mode is visible only to the owner and manager. It needs the number of classes to return, the amount, the method, and a mandatory reason. The confirmation dialogue shows the resulting balance before anything is written. The refund posts as a negative amount against revenue and the credits come straight off the balance.

退款退课模式只有 Owner 和 Manager 看得到。需要填退课节数、退款金额、退款方式和必填的退款原因；确认框会先列出退课后的余额变化再落库。退款以负数计入营收，退课节数直接从余额扣减。

Why your front desk cannot refund为什么前台不能退费

It is not an oversight and it is not a hidden button — the server refuses the request. A receptionist facing an upset parent should be able to say “I’m not able to do that, let me get the manager,” and have it be true. The rule moves the pressure of saying no off the person and onto the system.

这不是漏做，也不是把按钮藏起来——是服务端直接拒绝这个请求。前台面对情绪激动的家长时，应该能说出「这我做不了，我叫店长来」并且这句话是真的。这条规则把说「不」的压力从人身上挪到了系统上。

Everything shows up in the operation log

全部进操作日志

Thirty kinds of action are recorded with a plain-language name and a one-line summary: new student, archive, roster changes, class changes, uploads, share links, access codes, courses and packages, exports, publishing, team changes. Filter by student, type or date range. Owners and managers can export CSV. Records are kept for two years, then cleared automatically — export if you need longer.

30 类操作都以中文名称和一句可读摘要记录：建档、归档、排课变动、班次变动、上传、分享链接、访问码、课程与课包、导出、发布网站、团队变更。可按学员、类型、日期范围筛选；Owner/Manager 可导出 CSV。记录保留两年后自动清理，需要更久请自行导出。

05 · Student work学员作品

Private by default, published only on a recorded decision

默认私密；公开必须基于一次留痕的决定

Teachers, managers and owners can upload to a student’s portfolio: photo, title, date, note. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 30 megapixels per image — larger files are refused with a message rather than failing silently.

老师、店长和 Owner 都可以往学员作品集里上传：照片、标题、日期、说明。格式限 JPEG / PNG / WebP，单张最大 3000 万像素，超限会明确拒绝，不会静默失败。

A piece of work reaches the public gallery only if that student has a valid publication consent from the family. Withdraw the consent and every piece returns to private immediately — you do not have to hunt for them.

作品只有在该学员拥有有效的公开授权时才会进入官网作品墙。授权一旦撤回，所有作品立即回到私密状态——不需要你去一张张找。

Share links are temporary by design

分享链接被设计成临时的

A share link is a login-free page for one student’s work, for sending to relatives or using in recruitment. Owners and managers create them; the expiry is 1 to 90 days, 30 by default, and the link is shown once. Revoking is deliberately easier than creating — anyone who can edit the portfolio, including teachers, can kill a link. Cutting off something that escaped should never wait for a manager.

分享链接是某个学员作品的免登录页面，用于发给亲友或招生。创建仅限 Owner/Manager，有效期 1–90 天（默认 30 天），链接只显示一次。撤销的权限刻意比创建更宽——任何能编辑作品集的角色（含老师）都能撤。要切断一个已经外流的链接，不该等店长有空。

Share link or access code?分享链接还是访问码？

An access code is the family’s long-term key to their own records — name, phone and six digits, no expiry. A share link is a temporary public page of work only, opens with no details entered, and expires. Send a relative a share link; never send them an access code.

访问码是家庭长期查询自己记录的凭证——姓名、手机号、6 位数字，不设有效期。分享链接是只含作品的临时公开页，点开无需输入任何信息，会过期。给亲友发分享链接，绝不要发访问码。

06 · For families家长这一侧

The student area, and the code that opens it

学员专区，和那串打开它的数字

This section is the one to read out loud to a parent. There is no account, no password and nothing to install.

这一章是可以直接念给家长听的。没有账号、没有密码、不用装任何东西。

  1. The student must have a phone number on file first — without one the system refuses to issue a code.
  2. 学员必须先有手机号码，否则系统不会生成访问码。
  3. Open the student record, find Student area, press Generate. The six digits appear once. Copy them and hand them over in person or by private message.
  4. 打开学员档案，找到学员专区，点生成访问码。6 位数字只显示这一次，复制后当面或私信交给家长。
  5. The family enters name, registered phone and the code on the portal’s student area. They see credits left, next class, attendance history and their own work.
  6. 家长在门户「学员专区」输入姓名、登记手机号和访问码，即可看到剩余课时、下次上课、上课记录和自己的作品。

A lookup session lasts one hour, then asks for the three details again. Too many wrong attempts locks the code temporarily and says how long to wait. Regenerate invalidates the old code instantly and signs out anyone using it; disable closes self-service for that student entirely.

查询会话保持 1 小时，之后需要重新验证三项信息。连续输错会临时锁定并提示等待时间。更换访问码会立即作废旧码并踢掉已登录会话；停用则完全关闭该学员的自助查询。

“The parent says it doesn’t work”「家长说登录不上」

Almost always the name or phone number does not match the record exactly — most often because the record holds the parent’s number and the parent is trying their own child’s. Check the record, then regenerate if needed. The plain code cannot be looked up; nobody has it, including us.

绝大多数情况是姓名或手机号与档案里的不完全一致——最常见的是档案里存的是家长号码，而家长在填孩子的。先核对档案，必要时重新生成。访问码明文无法找回，任何人都没有，包括我们。

Consent records are permanent. Session data is cleared after 30 days, but a family’s publication consent and any withdrawal of it are never deleted — they are the proof of what was agreed and when.

授权记录永久保存。会话类数据 30 天后清除，但家庭的作品公开授权与撤回记录永不删除——那是「当初同意了什么、何时撤回」的凭证。

07 · Your team团队与权限

Five roles, enforced in the backend

五级权限，写死在后端

Only the owner adds, disables or re-roles a member (CMS → settings → team). Managers see the list and nothing more. Send temporary passwords over a channel you trust, and have people change them at first sign-in. Member count is capped by your subscription.

只有 Owner 能新增、停用成员或调整角色（CMS → 系统设置 → 团队与权限），Manager 只能看列表。临时密码请通过可信渠道转交，并要求对方首次登录后立即修改。成员数量受订阅套餐上限约束。

08 · Reports经营

What the numbers are actually telling you

这些数字到底在说什么

Owners and managers get a reporting tab: lifetime revenue, students enrolled, credits consumed, and the pool of credits still owed. Below it, twelve months of revenue and consumption, a six-month summary, a filterable financial detail report, and per-student spend.

Owner 和 Manager 有「经营统计」标签页：历史总营收、建档学员、累计消课、课时资产池；下方是近 12 个月的营收与消课、近 6 个月经营月报、可筛选的财务明细报表和单个学员的消费分析。

Money taken is not money earned收到的钱不等于赚到的钱

The dashboard separates them on purpose. A top-up is cash in and a liability — you owe those classes. It becomes revenue as the classes are taught. That is why “earned” and “prepaid but unused” are two different figures, and why a strong month of package sales is not the same as a strong month of teaching.

工作台刻意把两者分开。充值是现金流入，同时是一笔负债——你欠着这些课。它随着课上完才逐步变成收入。所以「已赚收入」和「预收未耗」是两个数，也所以课包大卖的一个月，不等于教学强劲的一个月。

Exports (student CSV, operation log CSV) need the export permission and are recorded in the audit trail. Your subscription also gates the feature — if it is not enabled, the interface says so and points you at us.

导出（学员 CSV、操作日志 CSV）需要导出权限，每次导出都写入审计。该功能同时受订阅套餐开关约束，未开通时界面会提示并指向平台方。

09 · Us平台方

What we can do inside your studio, and what stops us

我们能在你的工作室里做什么，以及什么拦着我们

We run the platform: creating studios, subscriptions and quotas, monitoring, backups, releases. We do not run yours.

我们负责平台：开通工作室、订阅与额度、监控、备份、发布。我们不负责经营你的工作室。

We cannot simply open your CMS. Every request into a studio is refused until a support session for that specific studio has been started with a written reason. Starting and ending a session, and everything done during it, is written to your audit trail — visible to the owner under Studio Admin → Analytics → Audit Trail, reason included. The session covers one studio; it does not carry over to another, and access is withdrawn the moment it ends.

我们不能直接打开你的 CMS。在没有为该工作室开启支持会话（含书面原因）之前，任何进入租户的请求都会被拒绝。会话的开启、结束以及期间的全部操作都写进你的审计记录——Owner 在品牌工作台「数据分析 → 操作审计」里可以看到，含我们填写的原因。会话只对一家工作室有效，不会顺延到另一家，结束即刻收回权限。

Isolation between studios is verified before every release: over 200 tenant-isolation checks must all pass or the release does not ship.

工作室之间的隔离每次发版前都会验证：两百多项租户隔离测试必须全绿，否则不允许上线。

Your subscription tier, its quotas and your billing dates are managed by us; they are read-only in your own screens. Ask and we will change them.

你的订阅档位、额度与账期由我们维护，在你自己的界面里是只读的。需要调整直接找我们。

11 · Questions常见问题

The ones we are actually asked

真的被问到的那些

I saved, but the website hasn’t changed.

我保存了，但网站没变。

Save Draft only stores a draft. Press Publish. If the image is the thing that did not change, also force-refresh your browser.

Save Draft 只存草稿，要点 Publish。如果没变的是图片，再强制刷新一次浏览器。

I published something wrong. Can I undo it?

发布错了，能撤回吗？

Yes. Preview / Publish → Publication history → Restore to Draft on the version you want, preview it, publish. Unpublished draft edits cannot be recovered this way.

可以。Preview / Publish → 发布历史 → 在想恢复的版本上点 Restore to Draft，预览确认后再发布。未发布过的草稿改动无法用这个方式找回。

Can I give a course an English name as well?

课程能不能同时有英文名？

No, and it is deliberate. Course names and work titles are operating data, stored once and shown as typed. Section headings — “Courses & Classes” and so on — are the bilingual part, set in Studio Admin.

不能，而且是刻意的。课程名与作品标题是运营数据，只存一份、按录入原样显示。双语的是板块标题（如「课程与班次 / Courses & Classes」），在品牌工作台里设置。

A parent wants to log in with an email and password.

家长想用邮箱密码登录。

Families cannot sign in to the CMS; the attempt is refused. Self-service is the student area with an access code — section 06.

家长不能登录 CMS，尝试会被拒绝。家庭自助走「学员专区」访问码——见第 06 章。

Uploads keep failing.

图片一直传不上去。

Check the format (JPEG, PNG, WebP) and the size — one image may not exceed 30 megapixels, roughly 6000 × 5000. Both limits are reported, never silent.

先看格式（JPEG / PNG / WebP）和尺寸——单张不能超过 3000 万像素，约 6000×5000。两种情况都会明确提示，不会静默失败。

Can I see whether a staff member issued a refund?

能查到某个员工有没有办过退款吗？

Owners can: Studio Admin → Analytics → Audit Trail, filtered by action. Refunds, exports, share links and our support sessions are all there, each with the person and the time.

Owner 可以：品牌工作台 → 数据分析 → 操作审计，按动作关键词过滤。退款、导出、分享链接以及我们的支持会话都在里面，每条带操作人和时间。

Two applications from the same phone number.

同一个手机号提交了两次。

Both cards carry the amber duplicate badge. Call before approving — see section 02.

两张卡片都会带琥珀色角标。批准前先打电话核实——见第 02 章。

Questions about hosting, backups, data location, multi-factor authentication or availability commitments are answered in the service FAQ — that one is written for the person signing the contract, this one for the person running the studio.

关于部署、备份、数据存放地、多因素认证或可用性承诺的问题，见服务 FAQ——那一份写给签合同的人，这一份写给运营工作室的人。
