Run your studio with PWE Studio

用 PWE Studio，把工作室运营得更从容

This guide covers v__APP_VERSION__, using Let’s Paint Studio as a worked example. It follows the rhythm of a real studio rather than the shape of a menu: what to set up first, what to do each day, and where to look when a result is not what you expected.

这份手册对应 v__APP_VERSION__，以 Let’s Paint Studio 为示例。它不按菜单罗列功能，而是按工作室真实的一周来讲：先准备什么、每天做什么、结果不对时先看哪里。

00 · Before you start开始之前

Four places to work, and three ideas that make the rest easier

四个工作入口，三个先弄懂就轻松很多的概念

Your studio has its own address on the platform: a short URL name called the slug. It is set when the studio is created, and every page below starts from it.

你的工作室在平台上有自己的网址标识，叫作 slug。它在开通时确定，下面每个页面地址都从它开始。

Place入口 Address地址 For适用对象
Portal 官网门户 /<slug> Visitors and families — your courses, work, enquiry form and student area 访客与家长——查看课程、作品、报名表和学员专区
Register 快速报名 /<slug>/register A focused enquiry page, ready to sit behind a QR code 专门用于报名咨询的页面，可直接放在二维码后
CMS 运营后台 /<slug>/cms Your team — students, classes, check-in, credits, work and approvals 你的团队——学员、班次、签到、课时、作品和审批
Studio Admin 品牌工作台 /<slug>/studio-admin The owner — brand, theme, website content and enquiry form Owner——品牌、主题、官网内容和报名表

Three ideas worth five minutes now

现在花五分钟，把三个概念放稳

1 · A credit is time, not a class

1 · 课时是时间单位，不等于一节课

A credit is prepaid teaching time. A longer class may use more than one, so families see a credit balance rather than a count of classes. Credits are added by top-up, deducted at check-in and refundable. A package is a bundle of credits for sale; plan means your subscription tier with PWE Studio and can only be changed by us.

课时是预付的教学时间。一节较长的课可能扣多个课时，所以家长看到的是「剩余课时」，不是「还能上几节课」。课时通过充值增加、签到扣减，也可以退回。课包是把课时打包售卖；套餐专指你与 PWE Studio 之间的订阅档位，只能由平台方调整。

2 · Families use a simple access code

2 · 家长用访问码，不用注册账号

There is no parent login. A family checks its own records with the student’s name + registered phone + a six-digit access code from your team. The code is shown once when it is created and is never stored in readable form. See section 06.

系统没有家长登录。家长用学员姓名 + 登记手机号 + 6 位访问码自助查询，访问码由你的团队生成后交给家长。明文只在生成时显示一次，系统不会以可读形式保存。见第 06 章。

3 · A draft is not the live website

3 · 草稿不是线上版本

In Studio Admin, Save Draft lets you prepare changes without changing what visitors see. Only Publish updates the portal, register page and CMS. Each publish keeps a version you can restore to draft.

在品牌工作台里，保存草稿让你先准备改动，不会改变访客看到的内容。只有发布才会更新门户、报名页和 CMS；每次发布都会保留一个可恢复为草稿的版本。

One language rule to remember记住这一条语言规则

Course names, work titles, addresses, phone numbers and people’s names are not translated. They appear exactly as your team entered them in both languages. The interface and section headings switch; operating data stays intact. A course called “周末水彩班” is a real name, not a sentence for a machine to rewrite.

课程名、作品标题、地址、电话和人名不翻译，两种语言下都按团队录入的原样显示。会切换的是界面文字和板块标题，运营数据保持原样。叫「周末水彩班」的课程，它就是一个真实名称，不是一句需要机器改写的话。

01 · Day one第一天

Build your front door before you welcome people in

先把官网准备好，再迎接家长和学员

Studio Admin is the owner’s workspace for the public face of the studio. Managers are guided to the CMS instead. That boundary is enforced by the server, so the right person owns the brand and the public promise.

品牌工作台是 Owner 管理工作室公开形象的地方。Manager 会被引导使用 CMS。这个边界由服务端执行，让品牌和对外承诺始终由合适的人负责。

  1. Open /<slug>/studio-admin and sign in with the email and temporary password from the platform. Change the password straight away (minimum 8 characters).
  2. 打开 /<slug>/studio-admin，用平台提供的邮箱和临时密码登录。请立即修改初始密码（至少 8 位）。
  3. Start with an industry preset. It gives you a first draft of the slogan, hero copy, enquiry questions, FAQ and theme. It overwrites existing content, so apply it before you begin writing. If you change your mind, use the Undo bar.
  4. 先选一个行业预设。它会一次生成 slogan、首屏文案、报名问题、FAQ 和推荐主题，帮你快速得到第一版。预设会覆盖已有内容，所以请在开始填写前使用；如果选错，可以点顶部的 Undo。
  5. Choose a starting palette. Eight curated themes are joined by Custom. Choose a colour or use From logo; PWE Studio takes the hue and solves the lightness and saturation for a readable page. Arcade Lime is dark only; unsupported modes are greyed out rather than approximated.
  6. 选一个配色起点。八张精选主题卡之外还有自定义。可以直接选颜色，也可以从 Logo 取色；PWE Studio 只取色相，再自动求解明度与饱和度，让页面保持清晰。街机青柠仅提供暗色；不支持的模式会置灰，不会勉强生成。
  7. Complete the studio details, hero, sections and enquiry questions. A section with no content stays hidden even when its switch is on, so you can prepare a page without showing an empty shell.
  8. 填写工作室信息、首屏、板块和报名问题。没有内容的板块即使打开开关也会保持隐藏，你可以先准备好，再放心发布。
  9. Save Draft, preview, then Publish. Preview the portal or register page in either language and at desktop or mobile width before you make it public.
  10. 保存草稿、预览，再发布。发布前可以在预览区切换门户 / 报名页、中英文以及桌面 / 手机尺寸。

Publish has a quality check for a reason

发布前的检查，是为了让页面站得住

Before publishing, PWE Studio checks three things: the studio name is present; the public essentials are complete (hero title, enquiry title and introduction, a phone number or email, and the principal’s name when that section is enabled — placeholder text does not count); and six contrast pairs across body text, secondary text and buttons each reach 4.5:1.

发布前，PWE Studio 会检查三件事：工作室名称已填写；公开内容完整（首屏标题、报名标题与引导语、电话或邮箱，以及开启主理人板块时的主理人姓名——占位文案不算）；正文、次要文字和按钮文字的六组对比度均达到 4.5:1。

The check that saves the most confusion最容易遇到的一道检查

“I saved, but the site did not change.” Save Draft keeps your work private; Publish makes it public. If Publish is stopped by Improve colour contrast before publishing, one of your custom colours is below 4.5:1. Return to the recommended theme or keep adjusting until the warning clears. A page people can read is always the better first impression.

「我保存了，网站怎么没变？」保存草稿会把改动留在后台，发布才会让访客看到。如果发布被「发布前请提高对比度」拦住，说明某个自定义颜色低于 4.5:1。可以回到推荐主题，也可以继续调整，直到警告消失。先让访客看得清，才是更好的第一印象。

Status colours adapt with the theme

状态色会随主题一起调整

The theme preview has two groups: six theme colours (page, panel, accent, support, control boundary and focus ring), plus three status colours (success, warning and danger) marked tuned to this theme. If a Custom accent comes too close to a status hue, PWE Studio moves the status colour and tells you why. Across the eight curated themes and their modes, 45 values are solved for readability and separation. A theme change updates them by design.

主题预览分两组：六个主题色（页面、面板、强调色、辅助色、控件边界和聚焦环），以及三个状态色（成功、警示、危险），后者标注「已按本主题调校」。如果自定义强调色接近某个状态色的色相，PWE Studio 会主动错开并说明原因。八张精选主题卡及其明暗模式共 45 个状态色值，都会针对可读性和区分度单独求解。换主题时它们一起更新，这是设计如此。

About the space

空间介绍

The Website tab includes About the space for the question behind the visit: “what will it feel like to be there?” Add a bilingual eyebrow, title and description, up to six photos and up to three highlights. A space photo opens the section; leaving both title and description empty keeps it private while you prepare.

官网标签页里的空间介绍，回答家长决定来访前最关心的问题：「来到这里会是什么感觉？」可以填写中英文小标题、标题和正文，上传最多六张空间照片，再加最多三条「空间亮点」。上传空间照片会打开这个板块；标题和正文都为空时，它仍保持隐藏，方便你先准备再发布。

Photos move gently for visitors and stop for anyone who prefers reduced motion. This is a separate story from the principal, courses, student gallery and your own Selected Work.

照片会在访客页面轻柔地轮播；开启「减少动态效果」的访客不会自动轮播。空间介绍与主理人、课程、学员作品墙和工作室作品是不同板块，各自讲一件事。

02 · Enrolment招生与报名

Turn a first hello into a clear next step

把第一次咨询，稳稳接成下一步

Families can enquire through the portal or the standalone register page. It is one form and one queue, arriving in the CMS Pending tab with a count in the navigation, so no new conversation gets lost between screens.

家长可以从门户或独立报名页发起咨询。两边使用同一张表、进入同一个队列，都会出现在 CMS 的待审核标签页，导航角标提醒你还有多少条需要跟进。

Pending has two labels: New enquiries and Class bookings. The navigation count is their total, while the tabs stay separate for a useful reason: approving an enquiry creates a student record; approving a booking requests a place in one class.

「待审核」页有两个标签：新报名和约课。导航角标显示两者总数，但页面分开处理：批准新报名会创建学员档案，处理约课则是为某个班次确认一个位置。

Each card keeps the useful context together: name, contact details, birthday, message, submission time, source (portal or quick register) and status. Statuses include pending, contacted, trial booked, following up, approved, enrolled, rejected, duplicate, lost and archived.

每张卡片都会把跟进需要的信息放在一起：姓名、联系方式、生日、留言、提交时间、来源（门户网站 / 快速报名）和状态。状态包括待审核、已联系、已约试听、跟进中、已批准、已建档、已拒绝、重复申请、已流失和已归档。

Approve when the next step is clear

确认下一步，再批准建档

Enter a starting credit balance (zero is fine) and press Approve. The student record and its opening credit entry are created together. If the enquiry is not a fit, reject it and the card leaves the active queue.

填入初始课时数（可以是 0），点批准建档，系统会同时创建学员档案并写入起始课时。若这条咨询不适合继续，点「拒绝」，它就会离开当前待处理队列。

The amber “possible duplicate” badge琥珀色的「疑似重复」角标

When two pending applications share a phone number, both cards are flagged. Pause before approving either one. Call the family: if it is a resubmission, keep the fuller card and reject the other; if two children share one parent’s number, approving both is right. Checking first prevents two records from splitting one family’s credit history. A separate warning, this phone already has a student, means the number matches an existing record.

两条待审核申请共用同一手机号时，两张卡片都会被标记。先别急着批准。请给家长打个电话：如果是重复提交，保留信息更完整的一条、拒绝另一条；如果是两个孩子共用家长号码，分别批准就是正确做法。先核对，可以避免同一个家庭的课时记录被拆到两份档案里。另有一条提示「此电话已有学员」，表示该号码与已建档学员重复。

Consent stays clear and traceable

家长同意了什么，始终清楚可查

The enquiry form requires a privacy consent tick, and stores the version of the privacy notice in force at that moment with it. Publication consent for student work is optional at this stage and recorded the same way. Both records are append-only: withdrawing consent creates a new event; it never rewrites the earlier one.

报名表必须勾选隐私同意，系统会把当时生效的隐私声明版本与同意记录一起存档；作品公开授权在这一步是选填的，记录方式相同。两类记录都只增不改：撤回授权会新增一条事件，不会改写之前的记录。

03 · Every day每一天

Make the day visible before class begins

上课前，先让今天一目了然

Set up a recurring class once — name, weekday, start time, length, capacity and students — and it appears on the right day each week. You can also add a student to one date by hand. The default time for new manual entries is a studio setting; changing it does not alter anything already saved.

固定班次只需设置一次——名称、星期、开始时间、时长、容量和学员——之后每周会自动出现在正确的日期。也可以把学员手动加入某一个日期。新建手动排课的默认时间属于工作室设置，修改它不会影响已经保存的排课。

A public timetable begins with a considered class record

公开课表，先从一份完整的班次资料开始

Owner and Manager can give a recurring class an optional course, teacher, room and public flag. The Public Timetable is a separate page at /<slug>/timetable, not another block on the portal. Turn it on in Studio Admin, choose 1–4 weeks, and separately decide whether to accept no-account booking. The number of weeks is also the booking window.

Owner 和 Manager 可以为固定班次关联可选的课程、授课老师和地点，并逐条设置是否公开。公开课表是独立页面 /<slug>/timetable，不是门户里的又一个板块。在品牌工作台打开页面并选择展示 1–4 周；是否接受免注册约课是另一个开关。展示周数同时就是约课时间窗。

  1. Course is optional. Create it in CMS → Settings → Course management: name, description, age range, duration and price. The last four are optional, and the schedule editor links to this area beside the course dropdown.
  2. 课程是可选的。在 CMS → 系统设置 → 课程管理里创建：名称、简介、适龄段、时长和价格，后四项均可选。排课编辑器的课程下拉旁边有直达链接。
  3. Archive, do not delete. Archiving removes a course from new scheduling while keeping every class, account and history that already refers to it. The confirmation shows how many classes currently use it.
  4. 归档，不是删除。归档后课程不再出现在新排课下拉里，但已经关联它的班次、账目和历史记录都会保留。确认框会告诉你目前有几个班次正在使用它。
  5. Public is per class and off by default. A private make-up slot or one-to-one lesson stays private even if it exists on the internal roster. A class may be public while its teacher’s name remains hidden until that teacher agrees in Team settings.
  6. 公开按班次逐条设置，默认关闭。内部补课或一对一时段即使存在于内部课表，也会保持私密。班次可以公开，但老师本人没有在团队设置里同意时，课表不会显示老师姓名。
  7. Cancel one occurrence, not the whole class. Use the class card’s cancellation for a holiday or missed date, add the reason, and restore it if plans change. The date must match the class’s actual weekday.
  8. 停某一天，不是停整个班。遇到公众假期或某次不上课，在班次卡片上停课并填写原因；计划改变时可以恢复。日期必须与这个班次实际授课的星期几一致。
  1. Open Daily roster; it starts on today.
  2. 打开每日排课，页面默认显示今天。
  3. Check students in as they arrive. Each check-in deducts the credits for that class and writes a ledger entry. Batch check-in skips anyone already checked in or without enough credits, and tells you who was skipped.
  4. 学员到场就签到。每次签到会扣减对应课时并写入账本；批量签到会跳过已签到或余额不足的学员，并明确告诉你谁被跳过。
  5. Checked in the wrong person? Reverse it. The credits return, attendance is marked reversed, and the ledger keeps the correcting entry. The history stays intact.
  6. 签错人了，可以撤销。课时会退回，考勤标记为已撤销，账本留下对应的冲正流水，历史记录不会被抹掉。

Two calendar exports, two different jobs两个日历导出，各有自己的用途

Weekly timetable ICS contains recurring classes and no student names; it is the shareable version. Today’s roster ICS contains the names of students attending and is for internal operations. Check which file you have before forwarding it.

固定课表 ICS只含周期班次、不含学员姓名，适合对外分享。当日 ICS含当天到课学员姓名，是内部运营文件。转发前先确认自己导出的是哪一个。

A student without enough credits is flagged at check-in, when it can still be handled kindly — not discovered at the end of the month. Birthdays in the next 14 days appear as a banner, and one tap copies a greeting with the student’s and studio’s names already filled in.

学员余额不足时，系统会在签到当场提示，让团队及时处理，而不是到月底才发现。未来 14 天内过生日的学员会显示横幅，点一下即可复制已经填好学员名和工作室名的生日祝福。

04 · Credits课时

Keep every payment explainable

让每一笔收款，都说得清楚

A student’s balance is the total of its ledger entries, not a number someone can quietly overwrite. Every movement records who made it, when and why. So when a family asks “how many credits are left?”, the answer comes from the record — not from anyone’s memory.

学员余额是所有账本流水的合计，不是某个可以被悄悄改掉的数字。每次变动都会记录操作人、时间和原因。所以家长问「还剩多少课时」时，答案来自记录，而不是谁的记忆。

Record a payment with confidence

把收款稳稳记入账本

  1. Search for the student. Their photo, current balance and last three movements appear together — take a moment to check them.
  2. 搜索并选中学员。头像、当前余额和最近 3 笔流水会一起显示，先花一点时间核对。
  3. Choose a package to fill in the credits and amount, or enter both yourself.
  4. 点「套餐快选」自动填入课时数和金额，也可以手动填写。
  5. Choose the payment method, add a useful note, and confirm. A ready-to-send confirmation message is available to copy in one tap.
  6. 选择付款方式，按需添加备注，然后确认收款入账。成功后可以一键复制确认话术发给家长。

Refunds deserve a second look

退费退课，需要多一次确认

Refund mode is visible only to the owner and manager. It asks for the classes to return, amount, method and a mandatory reason. The confirmation dialogue shows the new balance before anything is written. The refund is recorded as a negative revenue entry and the returned credits are removed from the balance.

退款退课模式只有 Owner 和 Manager 可见。需要填写退课节数、退款金额、退款方式和必填的退款原因。确认框会在写入前显示新的余额。退款会以负数记录在营收中，退回的课时会从余额中扣除。

Why the front desk cannot refund为什么前台不能退费

This is a permission boundary, not a missing button: the server refuses the request. When a parent asks for a refund, the receptionist can honestly say, “I’ll ask the manager to take care of that.” The system carries the boundary, so the person at the desk does not have to carry it alone.

这是权限边界，不是漏了一个按钮：服务端会拒绝这个请求。家长提出退费时，前台可以明确说「我请 Owner / Manager 来处理」。边界由系统承担，前台不需要一个人承受说“不”的压力。

The operation log keeps the thread

操作日志，把每件事串起来

Thirty kinds of action are recorded with a plain-language name and a one-line summary: new students, archives, roster and class changes, uploads, share links, access codes, courses and packages, exports, publishing and team changes. Filter by student, type or date range. Owners and managers can export CSV. Records stay for two years, then clear automatically — export what you need for longer.

30 类操作都会以中文名称和一句可读摘要记录：建档、归档、排课和班次变动、上传、分享链接、访问码、课程与课包、导出、发布网站和团队变更。可按学员、类型或日期范围筛选；Owner / Manager 可以导出 CSV。记录保留两年后自动清理，需要更久请及时导出。

05 · Student and studio work学员与工作室作品

Let the work speak — with consent and context

让作品说话，也把授权和边界说清楚

Your studio’s own Selected Work

工作室自己的 Selected Work

Selected Work is your studio’s own showcase. It answers “what kind of work and teaching can I expect here?” The student gallery answers “what can a student grow into here?” They have different authors, consent rules and jobs on the public site.

Selected Work 工作室作品是工作室自己的展示区，回答「这里的创作和教学是什么样？」；学员作品墙回答「在这里学习，能成长为什么样？」。两者的作者、授权规则和官网作用都不同。

  1. The public limit follows the plan: starter 15, studio 60, growth 150. It limits what is published, not how much you can keep and curate in the CMS.
  2. 官网展示上限按套餐：starter 15 件、studio 60 件、growth 150 件。限制的是公开展示数量，不是后台能保存和整理的数量。
  3. Downgrading never deletes work. Extra pieces stay editable in the CMS; the public page shows the first items in your chosen order. Upgrade again and the rest can return.
  4. 降级不会删除作品。超出当前上限的作品仍在后台，可以编辑和排序；官网只展示排序靠前的部分，升级后其余作品可以恢复展示。
  5. A work can have a photo, bilingual title and description, year, medium and category. Video is a link only: its cover uses your media quota, and the third-party player is not contacted until a visitor presses Play.
  6. 每件作品可以填写照片、中英文标题与说明、年份、媒介和分类。视频只保存链接，封面图占用你的媒体额度；访客按下播放前，页面不会向第三方网站发请求。

Student work stays private until consent is in place

学员作品，取得授权后才会公开

Teachers, managers and owners can add photo, title, date and note to a student’s portfolio. JPEG, PNG or WebP are accepted, up to 30 megapixels per image. Larger files are refused with a clear message.

老师、Manager 和 Owner 都可以往学员作品集里添加照片、标题、日期和说明。格式限 JPEG / PNG / WebP，单张最大 3000 万像素，超限会明确提示。

A piece reaches the public gallery only when that student has a valid publication consent from the family. Withdraw consent and every piece returns to private immediately; there is no need to find them one by one.

作品只有在该学员拥有有效的公开授权时才会进入官网作品墙。授权一旦撤回，所有作品会立即回到私密状态，不需要你一张张去找。

Share links are useful — and temporary

分享链接方便，但要有期限

A share link is a login-free page for one student’s work, useful for relatives or a recruitment conversation. Owners and managers create them; the expiry is 1 to 90 days, 30 by default, and the link is shown once. Revoking is deliberately easier than creating: anyone who can edit the portfolio, including teachers, can end a link. If a link needs to stop, it should not wait for a manager to become available.

分享链接是某个学员作品的免登录页面，适合发给亲友或用于招生沟通。创建仅限 Owner / Manager，有效期 1–90 天（默认 30 天），链接只显示一次。撤销的权限刻意比创建更宽：任何能编辑作品集的角色（含老师）都能结束链接。需要停止分享时，不必等 Manager 有空。

Share link or access code?分享链接还是访问码？

An access code is the family’s long-term key to its own records — name, phone and six digits, with no expiry. A share link is a temporary public page for work only; it opens without details and expires. Send a relative a share link, never an access code.

访问码是家庭长期查询自己记录的凭证——姓名、手机号和 6 位数字，不设有效期。分享链接是只含作品的临时公开页，点开无需输入任何信息，到期后失效。给亲友发分享链接，不要发访问码。

06 · For families家长这一侧

Give families a simple way to stay close

给家长一条简单、安心的自助路径

This is the part you can explain to a parent in one breath: there is no account, no password and nothing to install.

这一章可以直接讲给家长听：不用注册账号，不用记密码，也不用安装任何东西。

Public timetable and booking without registration

公开课表与免注册约课

When the studio turns it on, the portal navigation shows Timetable and opens the separate Public Timetable at /<slug>/timetable. A scheduled class is not automatically public: choose each class and choose which fields to show — teacher, room, age range, duration, capacity and price. A teacher’s name also needs that teacher’s own consent.

工作室打开功能后，门户导航会出现课程安排，进入独立的公开课表页面 /<slug>/timetable。排了课不等于自动公开：要逐条选择班次，也要选择显示老师、地点、适龄段、时长、容量和价格中的哪些字段。老师姓名还需要老师本人同意公开署名。

The timetable shows 1–4 weeks in the studio’s timezone, and the same number sets the booking window. Families see three clear seat states: Open (“N places left”), Nearly full and Full — waitlist available. A cancelled occurrence stays visible with its reason.

课表按工作室时区展示未来 1–4 周，这个数字同时决定约课时间窗。余位会清楚显示三种状态：还有 N 位、快满了、已满 · 可加候补。停课日期仍会显示，并写明原因。

Accept booking requests needs no account. A family enters the student’s full name and phone number, with an optional note. The request does not hold a seat; capacity is checked again when the studio approves it. If the same phone submits the same class twice, the page says “already received” instead of creating a second request. A full class can still accept a waitlist request.

约课不需要注册。家长填写学员全名和手机号，可选填备注。申请不会占座，工作室批准时才重新核对容量。同一个手机号对同一节课重复提交时，页面会提示「已经收到了」，不会生成第二条申请。已满的班次仍可提交候补申请。

  1. The student needs a phone number on the record first; without one, the system cannot issue a code.
  2. 学员档案里必须先有手机号码，否则系统无法生成访问码。
  3. Open the student record, find Student area and press Generate. The six digits appear once. Copy them and hand them over in person or by private message.
  4. 打开学员档案，找到学员专区，点生成访问码。6 位数字只显示这一次，复制后当面或私信交给家长。
  5. The family enters the name, registered phone and code on the portal’s student area. They can see credits left, the next class, attendance history and their own work.
  6. 家长在门户「学员专区」输入姓名、登记手机号和访问码，就能看到剩余课时、下次上课、上课记录和自己的作品。

A lookup session lasts one hour, then asks for the three details again. Too many wrong attempts temporarily lock the code and show how long to wait. Regenerate invalidates the old code immediately and signs out anyone using it; disable closes self-service for that student entirely.

查询会话保持 1 小时，之后需要重新验证三项信息。连续输错会临时锁定，并提示等待时间。更换访问码会立即作废旧码并让已登录会话退出；停用则完全关闭该学员的自助查询。

“The parent says it doesn’t work”「家长说登录不上」

Most often, the name or phone number does not match the record exactly. A common example is a parent’s phone being stored while the parent enters the child’s number. Open the record and check both fields. Regenerate the code only if they are correct and the problem remains. The plain code cannot be looked up — nobody has it, including us.

最常见的情况是姓名或手机号与档案里的不完全一致。比如档案里存的是家长手机号，家长却填了孩子的。先打开档案核对这两项；两项都对却仍然进不去，再重新生成访问码。访问码明文无法找回——任何人都没有，包括我们。

Consent records are permanent. Session data clears after 30 days, but a family’s publication consent and any withdrawal remain — they show what was agreed and when.

授权记录永久保存。会话类数据 30 天后清除，但家庭的作品公开授权与撤回记录不会删除——它们说明当初同意了什么、何时撤回。

07 · Your team团队与权限

Five roles, with the boundary behind them

五种角色，把边界交给系统

Only the owner adds, disables or changes a member’s role (CMS → Settings → Team and permissions). Managers can see the list, but not change it. Send temporary passwords through a trusted channel and ask each person to change theirs at first sign-in. Member count is set by your subscription.

只有 Owner 能新增、停用成员或调整角色（CMS → 系统设置 → 团队与权限），Manager 可以查看列表，但不能修改。临时密码请通过可信渠道转交，并要求成员首次登录后立即修改。成员数量受订阅套餐上限约束。

A teacher’s name is their choice

老师姓名是否公开，由老师本人同意

In Settings → Team and permissions, the owner can enable public display for each teacher and choose the name shown on the timetable. This is personal consent, not a layout preference. A class can be public while the teacher’s name stays hidden; being assigned to a class is not consent to publish a name.

在设置 → 团队与权限里，Owner 可以逐人打开老师的公开署名，并决定课表上显示的名字。这是个人授权，不是版式偏好。班次可以公开，但老师姓名仍然隐藏；被排进班次，不等于同意把姓名放到网上。

这不只是「不能编辑」，而是前台账号无法读取作品集。接待岗位不需要接触儿童照片；相关问题请转给老师或 Manager。

前台账号可以打开「待审核 → 约课」查看家长资料，但「批准」和「婉拒」只属于 Owner / Manager。请把申请转交给其中一人处理；目前权限边界就是这样设计的。

Staff 是早期的「全能员工」角色，接近 Manager，但没有退款、统计、导出和分享链接。新建成员时建议优先使用职责明确的角色——界面和岗位对得上，培训更容易，边界也更好解释。

08 · Reports经营

Read the story behind the numbers

读懂数字背后的经营

Owners and managers have a reporting tab that brings the shape of the studio into view: lifetime revenue, students enrolled, credits consumed and credits still owed. Below that are twelve months of revenue and consumption, a six-month summary, filterable financial detail and per-student spend.

Owner 和 Manager 可以在「经营统计」里看到工作室的整体节奏：历史总营收、建档学员、累计消课和仍待履约的课时。下方还有近 12 个月营收与消课、近 6 个月经营月报、可筛选的财务明细和单个学员的消费分析。

Money received is not the same as money earned收到的钱，不等于已经赚到的钱

The dashboard keeps the distinction visible. A top-up is cash received and a liability: the studio still owes those classes. It becomes revenue as teaching happens. So “earned” and “prepaid but unused” are different figures, and a strong month of package sales is not automatically a strong month of teaching.

工作台刻意把两者分开。充值是现金流入，同时也是一笔负债：工作室还要交付这些课。随着课程完成，它才逐步变成收入。所以「已赚收入」和「预收未耗」是两个数，课包卖得好，也不自动等于当月教学完成得好。

Exports (student CSV and operation log CSV) need the export permission and are recorded in the audit trail. Your subscription also controls availability; if the feature is not enabled, the interface tells you clearly and points you to us.

导出（学员 CSV、操作日志 CSV）需要导出权限，每次导出都会写入审计。该功能也受订阅套餐控制；未开通时，界面会明确提示并指向平台方。

你的订阅档位、额度和账期由我们维护，在工作室界面里是只读的。如果需要调整，直接联系我们处理。

11 · Questions常见问题

Questions that come up in real studios

工作室里最常遇到的问题

I saved, but the website hasn’t changed.

我保存了，但网站没变。

Save Draft keeps a private draft; only Publish changes the public site. If the image is the part that has not changed, force-refresh the browser after publishing.

保存草稿只保留后台草稿，只有发布才会改变公开网站。如果没变的是图片，发布后再强制刷新一次浏览器。

I published something wrong. Can I undo it?

发布错了，能撤回吗？

Yes. Go to Preview / Publish → Publication history, choose the version and select Restore to Draft. Preview it, then publish again. This does not recover edits that were never published.

可以。进入「预览与发布」→「发布历史」，找到需要的版本，点「恢复为草稿」，预览确认后再发布。没有发布过的草稿改动无法通过这里找回。

Can I give a course an English name as well?

课程能不能同时有英文名？

No, and that is deliberate. Course names and work titles are operating data: they are stored once and shown as entered. Section headings such as “Courses & Classes” are the bilingual part, set in Studio Admin.

不能，而且这是刻意的。课程名和作品标题属于运营数据，只存一份、按录入原样显示。会双语切换的是板块标题，例如「课程与班次 / Courses & Classes」，在品牌工作台里设置。

A parent wants to log in with an email and password.

家长想用邮箱密码登录。

Families cannot sign in to the CMS, and the attempt is refused. Their self-service route is the student area with an access code — see section 06.

家长不能登录 CMS，尝试会被拒绝。家庭自助请走「学员专区」访问码——见第 06 章。

Uploads keep failing.

图片一直传不上去。

Check the format (JPEG, PNG or WebP) and size — one image may not exceed 30 megapixels, roughly 6000 × 5000. The interface tells you which limit needs attention.

先看格式（JPEG / PNG / WebP）和尺寸——单张不能超过 3000 万像素，约 6000×5000。界面会明确提示是哪一项超限。

Can I see whether a staff member issued a refund?

能查到某个员工有没有办过退款吗？

Owners can open Studio Admin → Analytics → Audit Trail and filter by action. Refunds, exports, share links and our support sessions are recorded there, each with the person and time.

Owner 可以打开「品牌工作台 → 数据分析 → 操作审计」，按动作筛选。退款、导出、分享链接和我们的支持会话都会记录在这里，每条带操作人和时间。

Two applications from the same phone number.

同一个手机号提交了两次。

Both cards carry the amber duplicate badge. Call the family before approving either one — see section 02.

两张卡片都会带琥珀色角标。先给家长打电话，再决定是否批准——见第 02 章。

Why is the teacher missing from the public timetable?

为什么公开课表上没有老师名字？

A public class does not automatically publish a person’s name. The teacher must enable public display in Settings → Team and permissions. Ask the owner to check that individual consent switch.

公开班次不会自动公开个人姓名。老师必须在「设置 → 团队与权限」里打开公开署名。请 Owner 检查这位老师自己的同意开关。

I made a class public, but it is not on the timetable.

我勾选了公开，为什么课表上还是看不到？

Check three gates: the timetable page is on, the class has its own public flag, and its date falls inside the displayed weeks. Then check that the date is not cancelled and that the public fields you want to show are filled.

检查三道门：公开课表页面已打开、这个班次自己的公开开关已打开、日期在展示周数范围内。再确认当天没有停课，并检查希望显示的公开字段是否已填写。

Will downgrading delete my Selected Work?

降级会不会删掉我的工作室作品？

No. Downgrading only reduces how many pieces are published on the portal. Everything stays in the CMS and can return to the public selection after an upgrade.

不会。降级只会减少官网展示数量，后台作品不会被删除；升级后超出部分可以恢复公开。

Questions about hosting, backups, data location, multi-factor authentication or availability commitments belong in the service FAQ — that document is for the person signing the contract; this one is for the person running the studio.

关于部署、备份、数据存放地、多因素认证或可用性承诺的问题，见服务 FAQ——那份文档写给签合同的人，这一份写给运营工作室的人。
