# Pricing and Package Boundaries

Status: commercial draft for owner approval and signed order form
Currency: AUD
GST: treatment must be stated in the signed order form

## Subscription catalogue

| Plan | Monthly subscription | Students | Team users | Storage | Public showcase items |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Starter | $49 | 50 | 1 | 2 GB | 15 |
| Studio | $99 | 250 | 5 | 10 GB | 60 |
| Growth | $189 | 500 | 20 | 50 GB | 150 |

These figures match the current public plan catalogue and server-enforced limits. Core operations are not intentionally broken apart by tier; tiers primarily govern scale and team size. Xero is a separately granted paid add-on. The signed order form controls GST treatment, start date, minimum term and any negotiated inclusions.

## Included in the recurring subscription

- access to the subscribed PWE Studio product mode and plan limits;
- ordinary maintenance and security updates;
- customer-controlled role accounts within the plan limit;
- standard export functions already present in the product;
- reasonable support under the selected support tier;
- current documentation and release notes.

## Separately quoted

- initial discovery, configuration, branding and launch assistance;
- migration assessment, field mapping, data cleaning and exception handling;
- custom reports, features, integrations or workflow changes;
- additional storage, team users or tenants;
- on-site training or after-hours support;
- third-party hosting, messaging, payment or accounting provider fees;
- restoration work caused by unsupported customer changes or incomplete source data.

## Explicitly deferred from the current standard offer

- online payment processing and automatic financial reconciliation;
- automated SMS/email delivery providers;
- custom domains and DNS management;
- an off-instance backup copy, uptime monitoring, backup-failure alerting and a contractual SLA (AWS production hosting, daily database and media backups and a rehearsed restore are in place since 30 July 2026);
- organisation-level multi-campus aggregation.

Most messaging actions open the user’s own Mail or Messages application. A deployment may configure the logged SMTP email adapter, but no managed sending domain or SMS provider transport is included by default.

## Data migration boundary

PWE Studio provides the standard CSV and Excel templates, a mapping review and an exceptions list. We do not guarantee that an arbitrary customer CSV/Excel file is standardised, complete, internally consistent or legally authorised for import.

Migration work begins only after:

1. the customer supplies an authorised copy of the source data;
2. PWE Studio issues a mapping and exception report;
3. the customer approves the clean-up scope and cut-off time;
4. a backup/checkpoint and rollback method are recorded;
5. both parties approve the final import rehearsal.

## Multi-campus commercial rule

Each campus is a separate tenant and subscription. This gives each campus independent data, staff access, branding, operating settings, backup scope and support boundary. A future organisation layer may aggregate reporting and shared identity without weakening tenant isolation.

## Price changes and cancellation

The contract draft uses advance written notice for price changes and permits the customer to end the service before a change takes effect. This is intentional: Australian Consumer Law may protect small businesses from unfair terms in standard-form contracts. See the [ACCC contracts guidance](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/contracts).
