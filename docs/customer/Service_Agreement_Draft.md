# PWE Studio Service Agreement — Draft

> **Legal review required.** This is a plain-English commercial working draft, not legal advice and not ready for signature. An Australian lawyer should review the completed agreement, privacy roles, liability, consumer guarantees, GST, insurance and governing-law clauses.

## 1. Parties and order form

This agreement is between:

- **Provider:** `[full legal name]`, ABN `[ABN]`, of `[address]` (“PWE Studio”); and
- **Customer:** `[full legal name]`, ABN `[ABN]`, of `[address]` (“Customer”).

The signed order form records the selected plan, fees, GST treatment, service start date, initial term, tenant/campus count, authorised contacts, implementation scope and any negotiated variation. If the order form and this agreement conflict, the order form controls only for the expressly identified commercial item.

## 2. Service

PWE Studio grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the term to use the subscribed PWE Studio service for its internal studio operations.

The service includes only the capabilities, limits and support described in the order form and current customer documentation. Preview, demonstration, local-tunnel and beta capabilities are not production commitments unless the order form expressly says otherwise.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

The Customer must:

- keep account access individual and confidential;
- assign the minimum role needed for each staff member;
- promptly disable accounts when staff leave or change roles;
- ensure it has authority to collect, use, migrate and publish all supplied information and media;
- obtain and record appropriate parent/guardian authority for minors and publication consent;
- verify schedules, balances and migrated records before operational use;
- avoid uploading payment-card data, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information;
- maintain suitable devices, connectivity and third-party accounts;
- notify PWE Studio promptly of suspected unauthorised access or incorrect data.

The Customer remains responsible for its teaching services, child safety obligations, class decisions, refunds, accounting, tax, employment matters and communications sent from its device.

## 4. Fees and payment

The Customer pays the fees and taxes stated in the order form. Separately scoped implementation, migration, customisation, third-party fees and excess usage are not included in the recurring subscription.

PWE Studio may change a recurring price only on at least 30 days’ written notice. If the Customer does not accept the change, it may end the affected subscription before the new price takes effect without a price-change cancellation penalty.

No term excludes a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded, including an applicable consumer guarantee.

## 5. Data migration

PWE Studio will provide a standard CSV/Excel template, review the supplied structure and identify mapping exceptions. It does not promise that an arbitrary legacy file is complete, consistent, correctly authorised or ready to import.

No production migration occurs until both parties approve:

- source ownership and scope;
- field mapping and transformations;
- rejected/ambiguous records;
- cut-off and downtime window;
- backup, rehearsal, reconciliation and rollback evidence.

Customer approval of the reconciliation report is acceptance of the migrated dataset, subject to documented defects notified within `[10]` business days.

## 6. Privacy and security

Each party must comply with privacy, data breach, records and child-safety obligations that apply to it. The Customer determines which student/family information is collected and why; PWE Studio processes that information to provide and support the service.

PWE Studio will use reasonable technical and organisational measures described in the security schedule, including tenant isolation, role-based access, protected credentials, audit logging and private media handling.

If either party becomes aware of an incident affecting Customer data, it must notify the other without unreasonable delay, cooperate in containment and preserve relevant evidence. The parties will agree who leads any legally required notification, considering who has the direct relationship with affected individuals.

AWS hosting, production backup, recovery objectives and privileged-account MFA are pre-production items unless the order form expressly includes an accepted production service.

## 7. Customer data and intellectual property

The Customer retains its rights in Customer data and supplied brand material. The Customer grants PWE Studio the limited right to host, process, back up and transmit that material only to provide, secure and support the service.

PWE Studio retains its rights in the software, templates, documentation, designs, general know-how and improvements that do not identify the Customer or its students.

PWE Studio will not use Customer student media in public demonstrations or marketing without specific recorded authority. The professional demonstration tenant uses fictional records and synthetic media.

## 8. Third-party services

Cloudflare, AWS, email, messaging, accounting, calendar and payment services may be provided by third parties under their own terms and availability. PWE Studio is responsible for configuring included integrations with reasonable care but does not control a third party’s network or service.

The v8.0.0 device-native Mail/Messages feature opens the user’s application; the user decides whether to send. It is not automated provider delivery.

## 9. Availability, maintenance and support

No production availability commitment applies while the service is operating in the local + Cloudflare Tunnel stage.

When production AWS hosting is accepted, the order form should state:

- service hours and target availability;
- planned maintenance notice;
- support severity and response targets;
- backup frequency, retention, RPO and RTO;
- status and incident communication channels;
- exclusions for Customer systems, third-party outages and force majeure.

Support priorities and response targets are described in the Support Policy. Response time is not the same as resolution time.

## 10. Suspension

PWE Studio may temporarily restrict an account only where reasonably necessary to protect the service or people, investigate suspected misuse, comply with law, or address material unpaid fees after notice. Where practicable, PWE Studio will give notice, limit the restriction to what is necessary and restore access promptly when the reason is resolved.

## 11. Term and ending the agreement

The initial term and renewal method are stated in the order form. Either party may end for an uncured material breach after giving written notice and at least `[14]` days to remedy, unless urgent action is reasonably necessary to prevent harm.

The Customer may end a monthly subscription on `[30]` days’ notice. Any fixed-term early termination amount must reflect reasonable committed costs and must be reviewed for fairness before use.

After termination, PWE Studio will make a standard export available for `[30]` days, subject to payment and lawful retention. After that period it may delete or de-identify Customer data according to the deletion schedule, except where retention is required by law. Backup expiry may take longer and must be documented in the production backup schedule.

## 12. Warranties and liability

Each party warrants it has authority to enter the agreement.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, PWE Studio does not warrant uninterrupted operation or that the service will correct incomplete, inconsistent or unlawful Customer data. Nothing excludes statutory rights that cannot be excluded.

Any liability cap, indirect-loss exclusion, indemnity and insurance requirement must be completed by an Australian lawyer. The commercial intent is a balanced allocation that considers fees, control, insurance, data sensitivity and each party’s contribution—not an automatic one-sided exclusion.

## 13. Disputes

Before court proceedings, an authorised representative of each party will:

1. give written details of the issue;
2. meet in good faith within 10 business days;
3. attempt mediation in Victoria if not resolved within a further 15 business days.

This does not prevent urgent injunctive relief or a party using a statutory right.

## 14. Changes

Material changes to service scope, fees or this agreement require written notice. Where a change materially disadvantages the Customer, it may end the affected service before the change takes effect without a change-related penalty.

## 15. Governing law

The agreement is governed by the law of Victoria, Australia, subject to legal review and any non-excludable law.
