# Legal and Product Consistency Review — 1 August 2026

Status: internal review complete; external Australian legal sign-off required before first customer signature

This review checks the published customer material against the v8.1.1 product and current official Australian guidance. It is not legal advice and does not replace advice about PWE GROUP PTY LTD's circumstances or a customer's obligations.

## Outcome

The public privacy, terms, FAQ and support materials now describe the implemented product consistently. The most material factual defect was corrected: the weekly schedule ICS contains no identities, while the separately authorised daily roster ICS may contain student names. The support policy is now a public, stable page referenced by the terms and FAQ.

No customer agreement is ready for signature yet. The remaining items below require business particulars, a negotiated order form or an Australian lawyer; software changes cannot settle them.

## Binding-signature gates

1. Insert the provider's complete street/postal address and the customer's legal name, ABN and address.
2. Complete the order form: plan, GST, fees, term, cancellation notice, support hours, availability, backup retention, RPO/RTO, export window and deletion schedule.
3. Obtain Australian legal advice on Privacy Act coverage and roles, children's consent/records, liability cap, indirect loss, indemnities, insurance, consumer guarantees and Victorian jurisdiction.
4. Confirm whether each studio is subject to Victoria's Child Safe Standards and ensure its own child-safety policy, code, complaint/reporting path and staff controls exist. PWE Studio's software terms cannot substitute for the studio's operational obligations.
5. Execute and record an incident-response exercise, including who assesses serious harm and who notifies affected people and the OAIC where legally required.
